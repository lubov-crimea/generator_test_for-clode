The mobile site's cashier handles deposits, reached from the phone browser or from the home-screen shortcut. Three options are listed, PayNow, PayNow QR and DBS Bank, and each works in Singapore dollars.

| Method | Deposit time | Currency |
|---|---|---|
| DBS Bank | 1–2 minutes | SGD |
| PayNow QR | 1–2 minutes | SGD |
| PayNow | 1–2 minutes | SGD |

Currency (SGD) and deposit time (1–2 minutes) are the same in all three rows, which means the methods do not differ in speed.
