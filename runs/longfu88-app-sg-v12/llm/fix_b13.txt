Deposits are made in the cashier of the mobile website, in the phone browser or from the home-screen shortcut, with PayNow, PayNow QR or DBS Bank in Singapore dollars.

| Method | Currency | Deposit speed |
|---|---|---|
| PayNow | SGD | 1–2 minutes |
| PayNow QR | SGD | 1–2 minutes |
| DBS Bank | SGD | 1–2 minutes |

All three rows show the same currency, SGD, and the same deposit speed of 1–2 minutes, so the three methods do not differ in speed.
