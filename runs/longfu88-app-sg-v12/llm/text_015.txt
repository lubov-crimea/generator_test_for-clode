- **Is there a store app to download?** No, Longfu88 opens in the browser and is added to the home screen as a shortcut.
- **Which phones work?** Android, iOS and the mobile web of smartphones and tablets all work.
- **Is the shortcut required?** No, the same website opens in a browser tab.
- **How does a player open an account on a phone?** By e-mail, with the registration form of the Longfu88 website.
- **Which languages does support speak?** English, Malay, Simplified Chinese, Thai, Indonesian and Vietnamese.
- **Where to turn when something fails?** Customer support helps through live chat 24/7 or by e-mail.
