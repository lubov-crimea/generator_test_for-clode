Five steps open an account on the website, and the login screen gives access afterwards.

1. Select Register on the website.
2. Type an e-mail and set a password.
3. Tick the age box, and tick the Terms and Conditions as well as the Privacy Policy.
4. Fill out date of birth, legal name and the address of residence.
5. Press Create Account last, and complete any e-mail confirmation requested.

A player may open one personal account only and must be 18 or more. The legal gambling age set by the relevant jurisdiction applies too.
