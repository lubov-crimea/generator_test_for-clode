Four common phone problems with Longfu88 each have a plain fix:

- Shortcut missing: repeat the three steps in Chrome on Android or in Safari on iOS.
- Page not loading: reload the page and check the internet connection.
- Login failing: check the e-mail address and choose Forgot Password on the login screen.
- Problem beyond these checks: customer support helps through live chat, available 24/7, or by e-mail.
