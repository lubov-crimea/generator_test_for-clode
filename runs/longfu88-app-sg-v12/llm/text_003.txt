Players reach Longfu88 through the browser of a smartphone or tablet, so the only setup is a home-screen shortcut, added once. Nobody needs an installation file or a Longfu88 apk, because the casino opens in the mobile browser and sits on the home screen as a shortcut, with no store listing. The next two sub-sections give the steps for Android in Chrome and for iOS in Safari.

=== b04 ===
On Android phones and tablets, Chrome adds a shortcut of the casino website to the home screen in three steps.

1. Open the casino website in Chrome.
2. In the browser menu, choose Add to Home screen.
3. Confirm the shortcut.

The shortcut then appears among the apps of the phone.

=== b05 ===
Safari does the same on an iPhone or iPad: it adds a shortcut of the casino website to the home screen.

1. Open the casino website in Safari.
2. Tap Share and choose Add to Home Screen.
3. Confirm Add.

The new icon then sits on the home screen next to the other icons.
