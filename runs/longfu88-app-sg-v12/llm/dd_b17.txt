- **Is there a downloadable app in a store?** No. Longfu88 is used in the browser, with its shortcut sitting on the home screen.
- **Which devices can open it?** Android phones, iPhones and any other smartphone or tablet with a mobile browser.
- **Is making the shortcut mandatory?** No, a plain browser tab opens the same website.
- **How is an account created on a phone?** Through the registration form on the Longfu88 site, which requires an e-mail address.
- **Which languages can support answer in?** Simplified Chinese, Thai, English, Malay, Vietnamese and Indonesian.
- **Who helps when something fails?** Customer support, via e-mail or the live chat that never closes.
