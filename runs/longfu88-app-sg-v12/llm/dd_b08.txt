Withdrawals run through the cashier on the mobile site, in six steps.

1. Log in, then open the cashier.
2. Go to Withdrawal and pick where the money should land, among the supported destinations and payment methods.
3. Type the amount and name the receiving wallet, blockchain address or bank.
4. Finish identity and payment-method verification when it is prompted.
5. Check the request and submit it.
6. Wait for the casino to process it and for the bank or network to settle.

The casino usually processes requests inside three working days, and settlement of a bank transfer can add 3–5 business days. Before payments go out, KYC documents may be requested by the operator.
