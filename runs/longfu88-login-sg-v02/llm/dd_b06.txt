Six currencies are supported for accounts at Longfu88: SGD, MYR, THB, EUR, USD and USDT. A player's single personal account is kept in one of those currencies. The account also comes with three conditions:

- Provide accurate details at registration.
- Keep just one personal account.
- Never rely on a third party's payment method.

Put simply, an account is tied to one player and to that player's own means of payment, a rule that is easy for a newcomer to follow.
