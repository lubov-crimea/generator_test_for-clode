The Longfu88 register form is completed by e-mail, which is the only registration method offered, and it takes five steps in this order:

1. Open the site and select Register.
2. Enter an e-mail address and create a password.
3. Confirm age and acceptance of the Terms and Conditions and Privacy Policy.
4. Complete the additional personal details and the residential address.
5. Select Create Account and complete any requested e-mail confirmation.

Registration at Longfu88 requires an e-mail address, a password, a legal name, a date of birth and a residential address. The applicant must be 18 or older and of legal gambling age in the relevant jurisdiction, and only one personal account per player is allowed. A player who meets these rules can register and open the account.
