Customer support works through two channels: live chat, which is open 24/7, and e-mail at support@longfu88.com. Because the chat never closes, a question about a bonus activation or a pending withdrawal does not have to wait for office hours, while e-mail is also the route for self-exclusion requests. Support speaks six languages: English, Malay, Simplified Chinese, Thai, Indonesian and Vietnamese, so players from Singapore can ask in English or Simplified Chinese as well as in the other four.
