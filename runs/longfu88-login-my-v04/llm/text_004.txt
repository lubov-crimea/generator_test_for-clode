Verification is a check that the account holder is who the account says and that the payment details are theirs. The casino requests it before a withdrawal, during an account review, for unusual or high-value transactions and after changes to account or payment details.

| Document type | Accepted documents |
|---|---|
| Identity | Passport, national identity card or driving licence |
| Address | Utility bill or bank statement |
| Payment method | Bank or card ownership evidence, or digital wallet ownership evidence |
| Source of funds | Proof of income, bank statements or other supporting financial documents, only when requested |

Documents are due up to 30 days after the request, and a complete set is normally reviewed within up to 10 days of receipt.
