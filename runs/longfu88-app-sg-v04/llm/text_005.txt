Registration comes first and is done by e-mail only. The form asks for the e-mail address, a password, the legal name, the date of birth and the residential address. Once registered, the player logs in to the Longfu88 player account from a phone with the registered e-mail and password, exactly as on a computer: select Login on the website, enter both details and complete any security check shown. A player who forgot the password chooses Forgot Password on the login screen.
