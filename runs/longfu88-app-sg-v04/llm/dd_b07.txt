Longfu88 accepts registration by e-mail alone, and this comes first. Signing up needs the birth date, the legal name, a residential address, an e-mail and a chosen password. A registered player signs in from a phone exactly as from a computer: the Login option is selected on the website, the e-mail and password of the account are typed in, and any security check shown is completed. Someone who forgets the password can open Forgot Password from the screen used to sign in.
