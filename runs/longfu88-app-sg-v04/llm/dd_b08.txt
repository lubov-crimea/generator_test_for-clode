A phone can enter Longfu88 either through a browser tab or via the shortcut icon that sits on the home screen, and both open the same responsive site. Setting up the shortcut happens once and results in an extra icon, while the tab needs no preparation. For a phone used every day the shortcut is handier, and for a device that rarely opens Longfu88 a tab is enough.
