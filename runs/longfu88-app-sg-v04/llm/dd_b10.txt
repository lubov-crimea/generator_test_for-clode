Longfu88 customer support covers whatever the checks below leave open. Four typical phone problems and their fixes:

- Shortcut absent: run through the steps again, Chrome for Android and Safari for iOS.
- Page does not open: reload it, then check that the internet connection is working.
- Entry refused at sign-in: look over the typed e-mail address, and otherwise use Forgot Password on that screen.
- Still stuck: write to support by e-mail, or open the live chat (24/7).
