Registration is by e-mail only, in five steps.

1. Open the site and select Register.
2. Enter e-mail and create a password.
3. Confirm age and acceptance of Terms and Conditions and Privacy Policy.
4. Add the remaining personal details and residential address.
5. Select Create Account and complete any e-mail confirmation.

Registration requires e-mail, password, legal name, date of birth and residential address, and each player may hold one personal account, if 18+ and of legal gambling age in the relevant jurisdiction.
