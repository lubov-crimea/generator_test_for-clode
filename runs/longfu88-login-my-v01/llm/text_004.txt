The casino may request these documents before a withdrawal, during an account review and for unusual or high-value transactions.

| Document | Accepted |
|---|---|
| Identity | Passport, national identity card, driving licence |
| Address | Utility bill, bank statement |
| Payment method | Bank or card ownership evidence, digital wallet ownership evidence |
| Source of funds, if requested | Proof of income, bank statements, other supporting financial documents |

A complete document set is normally reviewed within up to 10 days. The account holder is responsible for the login credentials, while technical and organisational safeguards protect accounts, payments and personal information.
