Age comes first: the policy sets a minimum of 18 years, and gambling is allowed solely where lawful. Self-assessment questions let players notice possible gambling-related harm early, as a checkpoint. Closing an account can be requested through customer support, and self-exclusion can place limits on the account and on gambling during the agreed period. Outside help is offered by the independent organisations named in the policy: GamCare, Gambling Therapy and Gamblers Anonymous.
