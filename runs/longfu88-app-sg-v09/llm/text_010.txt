The policy sets one rule first: players must be at least 18 years old and gamble only where lawful. Self-assessment questions help players identify possible gambling-related harm, so they work as an early checkpoint. A player who wants a break can request account closure through customer support, and self-exclusion may restrict account access and gambling for the agreed period. For outside help, the independent organisations named are GamCare, Gamblers Anonymous and Gambling Therapy.
