Registration on the phone is one form, completed in five steps.

1. Open the website and select Register.
2. Enter an e-mail and create a password.
3. Confirm age and accept the Terms and Conditions and Privacy Policy.
4. Complete the additional personal details and residential address.
5. Select Create Account and complete any requested e-mail confirmation.

The form asks for an e-mail, a password, a legal name, a date of birth and a residential address. Players must be 18 or over, legally allowed to gamble where they live, and hold one personal account only.
