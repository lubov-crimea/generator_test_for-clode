Four quick checks handle the common problems met on the Longfu88 website from a phone:

- Shortcut missing: go through the Android Chrome or iOS Safari steps again.
- Page not loading: refresh the page and make sure the internet connection is working.
- Login failing: recheck the e-mail address entered, or choose Forgot Password from the login screen.
- Problem remaining: customer support, reachable by e-mail or by live chat that runs 24/7, also covers what the checks above leave open.
