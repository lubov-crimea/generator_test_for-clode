Opening an account on the phone takes one form and five steps.

1. Go to the website and press Register.
2. Supply an e-mail and decide on a password.
3. Confirm age, then agree to the Privacy Policy as well as the Terms and Conditions.
4. Add the residential address and other personal details.
5. Press Create Account to finish, and act on the e-mail confirmation if one arrives.

Beyond the e-mail and password, the form takes the residential address, the birth date and a legal name. Eligibility means being 18 or over, gambling lawfully where one lives, and holding one personal account only.
