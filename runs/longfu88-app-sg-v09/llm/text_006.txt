Verification may be requested during an account review, before a withdrawal, after unusual or high-value transactions and when account or payment details change. The set of documents is short:

- Government-issued photo ID or passport
- Proof of residential address
- Proof of ownership of a payment method
- Source of funds or wealth documents when requested

Documents are submitted within up to 30 days when requested, and a complete set is typically reviewed within up to 10 days after it is received.
