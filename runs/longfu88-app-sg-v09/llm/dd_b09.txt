Because the cashier lives inside the mobile website, withdrawals begin in the phone browser and take six steps.

1. Sign in, then open the cashier.
2. Choose Withdrawal, then the destination or payment method from the supported options.
3. Set the amount, and name where it should arrive: bank, wallet or blockchain address.
4. When the system prompts it, identity and payment-method checks follow.
5. Look the request over, then submit it.
6. After the casino processes it and any settlement by the bank or network, the funds arrive.

In general, three working days cover processing, and for a bank transfer 3-5 further business days may be needed. KYC documents may be asked for by the operator ahead of processing.
