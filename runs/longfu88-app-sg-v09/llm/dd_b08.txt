Account reviews, withdrawals, high-value or otherwise unusual transactions and altered account or payment details are the moments when verification may be requested. The short document set is:

- Photo ID from a government authority, or a passport
- A document proving the home address
- If requested, documentation on how the funds or the wealth were obtained
- Evidence of the player's ownership of the payment method

Up to 30 days are allowed for sending in requested documents, and once they are all in, review normally takes 10 days at most.
