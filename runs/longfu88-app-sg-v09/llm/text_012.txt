Four quick checks cover the usual problems with the Longfu88 website on a phone:

- Shortcut missing: repeat the steps in Chrome on Android or in Safari on iOS.
- Page not loading: reload it and check the internet connection.
- Login failing: check the e-mail address and choose Forgot Password on the login screen.
- Problem remaining: contact customer support through the 24/7 live chat or by e-mail, which also covers what the earlier checks do not solve.
