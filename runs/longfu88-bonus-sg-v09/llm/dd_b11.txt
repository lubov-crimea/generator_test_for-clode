Tiers of the VIP Program, five in all: Bronze, Silver, Gold, Platinum, Diamond. VIP points come from real-money gameplay that is eligible, and Longfu88 turns them into a ladder of 25 numbered levels with rewards. A points threshold and a level reward belong to every level. For instance, 30 points mean level 3 and a reward of €14.7, and 300 points reach level 8, which pays €73.5. The table covers eight of the 25 levels, namely 1, 3, 5, 8, 10, 15, 20 and 25, and the reward column shows what each of them pays.

| Level | Level reward | Points required |
|---|---|---|
| 25 | €980 | 5500 |
| 20 | €637 | 3600 |
| 15 | €392 | 2100 |
| 10 | €147 | 700 |
| 8 | €73.5 | 300 |
| 5 | €24.5 | 70 |
| 3 | €14.7 | 30 |
| 1 | €9.8 | 1 |

Rewards grow with the level: level 1 pays €9.8 and level 25 pays €980, while the points climb from 1 to 5500 across the same span. Between level 10 at 700 points and level 15 at 2100 points, the reward rises from €147 to €392, and level 20 sits at 3600 points and €637. Levels 1 and 25 mark the lowest and the highest step of the ladder. A personal VIP manager is listed as well, available to the high tiers that are eligible, and the benefits include VIP cashback and personalised offers, while withdrawal limits are higher. Reward amounts and withdrawal limits depend on the level and on the currency, and benefits tied to a level can change under the program rules, so a player who plans around one level checks the current program terms first.
