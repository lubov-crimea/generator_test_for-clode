At Longfu88 the rewards area adds game-like features to the bonuses and groups them under the Rewards menu, so the path of each feature is part of its description. Coins connect several of the features: the Wheel of Fortune accepts them for a spin and also lists them among its prizes, and the Coin Shop exchanges earned Coins for rewards. Five features make up the area:

- Bonus Crab: claim qualifying picks to open a crab reward, valid 7 days after the reward is assigned.
- Wheel of Fortune: spend an eligible spin or Coins to rotate a prize wheel with bonus credits, Free Spins or Coins as prizes, valid 7 days.
- Challenges: complete qualifying gambling tasks to unlock listed rewards, found under Rewards > Challenges.
- Collections: collect campaign items by completing qualifying activity, found under Rewards > Collections.
- Coin Shop: exchange earned Coins for reward items and bonus credits up to €4,500, found under Rewards > Shop.

Rewards may carry separate wagering requirements, so the terms of a reward are read next to the reward itself. The first feature of the list, Bonus Crab, shows how those numbers look in practice, and the sub-section below follows it step by step.

Bonus Crab turns qualifying picks into prizes: the player claims the picks to open a crab reward and reveal a casino prize, and qualifying activity in the rewards area may grant free picks. The feature sits next to the Wheel of Fortune, Challenges, Collections and the Coin Shop, and the crab reward it opens comes in three kinds of prize, as the list below shows:

- Bonus credits
- Free Spins
- Coins

Bonus Crab awards these prizes at Longfu88, and a crab reward is valid for 7 days after the reward is assigned. Wagering depends on the kind of prize: 30x the awarded bonus credit and 40x the winnings from Free Spins, so each of the two has its own multiple to clear.
