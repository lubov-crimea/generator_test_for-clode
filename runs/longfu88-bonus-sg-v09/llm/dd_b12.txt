The rewards area at Longfu88 sits in the Rewards menu and brings game-like features on top of the bonuses, so where a feature is found matters as much as what it does. Coins tie several features together: the Wheel of Fortune takes them for a spin and also lists them as a prize, and the Coin Shop turns earned Coins into rewards. The area has five features, and rewards from Wheel of Fortune as well as from Bonus Crab are valid for 7 days.

- Bonus Crab: qualifying picks, once claimed, open a crab reward, and the reward is valid for 7 days from assignment.
- Wheel of Fortune: spinning requires Coins or an eligible spin, and the wheel pays bonus credits, Free Spins or Coins, with 7 days of validity.
- Challenges: gambling tasks that qualify open up listed rewards, under Rewards > Challenges.
- Collections: items from campaigns are collected as qualifying activity is completed, under Rewards > Collections.
- Coin Shop: earned Coins buy reward items and bonus credits, with the credits reaching €4,500, under Rewards > Shop.
