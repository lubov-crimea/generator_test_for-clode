The VIP Program has five tiers: Bronze, Silver, Gold, Platinum and Diamond. A player earns VIP points through eligible real-money gameplay, and at Longfu88 those points unlock numbered reward levels, 25 in all. Each level has a points threshold and a level reward, so progress is easy to read: 30 points, for example, correspond to level 3 with a reward of €14.7, while 300 points reach level 8 and €73.5. The table below lists eight of the 25 levels, namely 1, 3, 5, 8, 10, 15, 20 and 25.

| Level | Points required | Level reward |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | 1 | €9.8 |
| 3 | 30 | €14.7 |
| 5 | 70 | €24.5 |
| 8 | 300 | €73.5 |
| 10 | 700 | €147 |
| 15 | 2100 | €392 |
| 20 | 3600 | €637 |
| 25 | 5500 | €980 |

The reward grows with the level: level 1 carries €9.8 and level 25 carries €980, while the points required climb from 1 at the first level to 5500 at the last. Further up the table, 2100 points correspond to level 15 with €392, and 3600 points correspond to level 20 with €637. Levels 1 and 25 are the first and the last step of the whole ladder. Beyond the level rewards, the VIP Program lists personalised offers, VIP cashback, higher withdrawal limits and, for eligible high tiers, a personal VIP manager as additional benefits. Reward and withdrawal limits may differ by level and currency, and level-based benefits can change under the program rules, so a player who plans around a particular level checks the current terms of the program first.
