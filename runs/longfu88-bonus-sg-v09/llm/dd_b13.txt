Qualifying picks are what Bonus Crab runs on. A player claims them to open a crab reward, which reveals a casino prize. Free picks can be earned through activity that qualifies, anywhere in the rewards area. The feature therefore combines two things: picks as the entry and a prize that comes out of the crab reward. Next to the Coin Shop, Challenges, Collections and Wheel of Fortune, it offers three kinds of prize:

- Bonus credits
- Free Spins
- Coins

Those prizes are awarded through Bonus Crab at Longfu88, and a crab reward carries a validity of 7 days from assignment. The wagering depends on the prize: awarded bonus credit has to be wagered 30x, and Free Spins winnings 40x, so two separate multiples apply. Because the kinds of prize differ, the wagering differs as well.
