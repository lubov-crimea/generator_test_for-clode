Longfu88 asks for verification at certain moments: when an account is reviewed, ahead of a withdrawal, whenever a transaction stands out as unusual or very large, and when account details or payment details change. Registration and login do not involve it. Having the papers close at hand on the same phone saves time once a request comes in. These are the documents accepted for each purpose:

- Identity: driving licence, national identity card or passport.
- Address: utility bills qualify, and so do bank statements.
- Payment method: digital wallet ownership evidence, or bank or card ownership evidence.
- Source of funds: supporting financial documents, for instance bank statements or evidence of income, required only on request.

Documents are due within the 30 days that follow the request. A complete set normally gets a review within 10 days at most, with the clock starting when the last missing paper comes in.
