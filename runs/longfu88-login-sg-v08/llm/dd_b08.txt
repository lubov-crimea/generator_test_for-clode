A first withdrawal from a phone brings the account under close inspection. Withdrawals can trigger identity and payment checks, and funds can be held back until three things are settled: the player's identity, who owns the payment method used and, where required, the source of funds. A withdrawal therefore waits for that confirmation. As a rule, Longfu88 processes withdrawal requests within three working days, a figure that describes usual handling and carries no promise of a payout time. Only payment methods owned by the player are accepted, and those of third parties are excluded. The papers prepared in the section above, kept on the same phone, make completing these checks simpler.
