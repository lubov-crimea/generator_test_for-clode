On a phone, as anywhere else, the account holder is responsible for safeguarding the login credentials. A registered e-mail and a password belong with the person who registered, so a device that player controls is the right place to type them. Operator duties come on top. Longfu88 applies technical and organisational safeguards that protect accounts, payments and personal information. It may also suspend activity for verification or suspected abuse, so a pause on an account can follow a verification request. Account security therefore rests on both sides.
