Keeping the login credentials safe is the account holder's own task, and a phone does not change that. The e-mail and password belong to whoever registered them, which makes a device under that person's control the sensible place to enter them. Duties lie with the operator too. Longfu88 applies technical and organisational safeguards covering personal information along with payments and accounts, and it can suspend activity if verification is called for or if abuse is suspected, a step that may leave an account paused after a verification request. Security thus rests on both sides.
