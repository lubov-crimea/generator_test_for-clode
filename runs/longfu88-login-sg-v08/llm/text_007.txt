A first withdrawal from a phone is the moment when the account is looked at closely. Identity and payment checks may be required for withdrawals, and payments may be held until identification, payment ownership and any required source-of-funds checks are complete. So a withdrawal waits for those checks before it moves on. At Longfu88, requests are generally processed within three working days, which describes the usual handling and is not a promise of a payout time. Payment methods must belong to the player, and third-party payment methods are not used. Documents from the previous section, kept ready on the phone, make the checks easier to finish.
