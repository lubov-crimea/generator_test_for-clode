At Longfu88, account verification is requested on set triggers: during an account review, before a withdrawal, for unusual or high-value transactions and after changes to account or payment details. It is not a step of registration or login. Keeping the papers within reach on the same phone helps when a request arrives. The list shows which documents count for each purpose:

- Identity: passport, national identity card or driving licence.
- Address: utility bill or bank statement.
- Payment method: bank or card ownership evidence, or digital wallet ownership evidence.
- Source of funds: proof of income, bank statements or other supporting financial documents, needed only when requested.

Documents are due up to 30 days after the request. A complete set is normally reviewed within up to 10 days, so the review time starts when the last missing paper arrives.
