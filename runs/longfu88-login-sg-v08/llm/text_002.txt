=== b02 ===
Logging in takes the registered e-mail address and a password, and a Longfu login looks the same on a phone, a tablet and a computer. A phone browser opens the Longfu88 website just as a computer does, and the Longfu88 login screen asks for the same two details. The player enters the registered e-mail and password on that screen, in three steps:

1. Select Login on the website.
2. Enter the registered e-mail and password.
3. Complete any security check and access the account.

A security check, where one appears, comes before access to the account, so the account opens only after the check is passed.

=== b03 ===
A player who forgot the password sets a new one from the login screen in a phone browser. Longfu88 works through the registered e-mail here: the player requests a reset link, and the e-mailed instructions lead to the new password.

1. Choose Forgot Password on the login screen.
2. Request a reset link using the registered e-mail.
3. Follow the e-mailed instructions to set a new password.

A player who no longer has access to the registered mailbox should contact support.
