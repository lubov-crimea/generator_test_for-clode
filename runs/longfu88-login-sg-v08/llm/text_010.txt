Customer support answers account, login and verification questions through live chat and e-mail. Live chat runs 24/7, which suits a problem met on a phone at any hour, and the e-mail address is support@longfu88.com. Typical cases are a login that fails, a reset e-mail that does not arrive or a document upload that needs help. Support speaks English, Malay, Simplified Chinese, Thai, Indonesian and Vietnamese, so a player can write in the language that feels easiest.
