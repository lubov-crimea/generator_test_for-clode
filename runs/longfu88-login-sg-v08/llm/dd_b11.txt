Customer support takes questions about an account, a sign-in or verification by e-mail, and also in live chat. The live chat service never closes, 24/7. Typical reasons to write in include trouble signing in, a reset e-mail that goes missing, or help with uploading a document. E-mails go to support@longfu88.com. Support speaks Malay, Indonesian, Vietnamese, Thai, English and Simplified Chinese, which lets a player write in whichever feels easiest.
