Four situations cover most phone problems with Longfu88, and each one has a simple first remedy to try ahead of contacting support:

- Shortcut not visible: redo the home-screen steps with Chrome for Android phones, Safari for iPhones and iPads.
- Page stuck: reload it and look at the internet connection.
- Sign-in refused: confirm that the e-mail address was typed correctly, then reset the password through Forgot Password, which the login screen offers.
- Still unsolved: contact customer support, where live chat runs day and night and e-mail is accepted.
