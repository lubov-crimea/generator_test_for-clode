The player account is the entry point to everything else on a phone. Registration is by e-mail only, and the form asks for these details:

- E-mail
- Password
- Legal name
- Date of birth
- Residential address

Login on Longfu88 is the same as on a computer: select Login, then enter the registered e-mail and password. Anyone who forgot the password uses Forgot Password on the login screen, as the login page explains.
