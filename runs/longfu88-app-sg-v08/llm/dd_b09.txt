Betting features sit in the sportsbook of the mobile website, and these sports are covered:

- Cricket
- Football
- Badminton
- Esports
- Tennis
- Volleyball
- Basketball
- Virtual football
- Table tennis

Bet types include Bet Builder and outrights, totals and handicap, live bets, accumulators and singles. Live betting brings live odds along with in-play markets, but which events are offered and which markets can be picked differs between jurisdictions.
