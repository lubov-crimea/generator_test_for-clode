Most phone problems with Longfu88 fall into four cases, and each has a plain first fix before support is needed:

- Shortcut missing: repeat the steps in Chrome on Android or in Safari on iOS.
- Page not loading: reload it and check the internet connection.
- Login failing: check the e-mail address and use Forgot Password on the login screen.
- Problem remaining: contact customer support through the 24/7 live chat or by e-mail.
