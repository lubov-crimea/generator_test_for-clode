The player account is where everything on a phone begins, from games to bonuses. Registration needs only an e-mail, and the form collects these details:

- Date of birth
- Legal name
- Password
- Residential address
- E-mail

Logging in to Longfu88 mirrors the desktop routine: select Login, then supply the account's e-mail address and password. The login screen has a Forgot Password option for lost passwords, and the login page explains how it works.
