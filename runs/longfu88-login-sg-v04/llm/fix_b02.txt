Registration at Longfu88 runs by e-mail only and takes five steps, from the Register button to the confirmation of the new account.

1. Open the site and select Register.
2. Enter an e-mail address and create a password.
3. Confirm age and acceptance of the Terms and Conditions and the Privacy Policy.
4. Complete the additional personal details and the residential address.
5. Select Create Account and complete any requested e-mail confirmation.

Registration requires an e-mail address, a password, a legal name, a date of birth and a residential address, so the form collects identity details as well as login data. Eligibility has two conditions: the player is 18 or over and of legal gambling age in the relevant jurisdiction, and each player holds only one personal account. Once the account exists, the Longfu login uses the same e-mail and password that were entered in the second step.
