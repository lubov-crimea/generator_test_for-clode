Credentials are looked after by the account holder: the e-mail used for the account and its password must stay private. As these two items make up the whole login, their privacy is the main thing a player can do for account security. The operator speaks of its own role only in general terms. At Longfu88, technical and organizational measures are applied to accounts, payments and personal information. Account activity may be suspended as well, should verification or suspected abuse call for it. Personal data can be passed to payment service providers, to identity verification and technology providers, and to regulators and law enforcement if the law demands. Players have these rights over their data: withdrawal of consent, objection, restriction, access, rectification, portability where applicable and erasure where legally permitted.
