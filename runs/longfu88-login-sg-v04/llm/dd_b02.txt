New accounts at Longfu88 are created by e-mail alone, in five steps that run from the Register button to the confirmed account.

1. Go to the site and click Register.
2. Give an e-mail and pick a password.
3. Confirm age, agree to the site Terms and Conditions, and read through the Privacy Policy.
4. Provide further personal details plus the residential address.
5. Finish with Create Account, then complete whatever e-mail confirmation is requested.

Besides the login pair, the form gathers the player's legal name, birth date and place of residence, so identity data is collected at sign-up as well. Eligibility rests on two conditions. One is age: the player is of legal gambling age under the relevant jurisdiction and at least 18. The other is that every person keeps a single personal account. After sign-up, the Longfu login accepts the e-mail and password typed in the second step.
