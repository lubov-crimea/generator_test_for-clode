Two settings stay with the player at Longfu88, and each covers a different part of the experience. The first is marketing: players may manage direct-marketing preferences and unsubscribe from promotional communications, for example messages about bonus offers. Cookies are the second area of control. Necessary, preference, analytics and marketing cookies support website functions and measurement. The first setting decides what reaches the player, while the second concerns how the site works and how its use is measured.
