The account holder is responsible for safeguarding the login credentials, that is the e-mail address and the password that open the account. Because the login consists of these two details alone, keeping both private is the player's own part of account security. On its side, the operator describes its part at a general level: Longfu88 applies technical and organizational safeguards to accounts, payments and personal information. Activity may also be suspended for verification or suspected abuse. Personal data may be shared with payment service providers, identity verification providers and technology providers, and with regulators and law enforcement when required. Players hold the following rights over their personal data: access, rectification, erasure where legally permitted, restriction, objection, portability where applicable and withdrawal of consent.
