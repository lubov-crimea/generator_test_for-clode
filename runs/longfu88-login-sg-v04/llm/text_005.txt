A player of Longfu88 who wants to stop contacts customer support and asks for self-exclusion or account closure. Self-exclusion means that account access and gambling may be restricted for the agreed period, while closure is requested through customer support in the same way. Both options therefore start with one request, and the choice between a restriction and an ended account is made in it. Players must be at least 18 and gamble only where lawful. The procedure has four steps:

1. Contact support and request self-exclusion or account closure.
2. Confirm the account details and the preferred exclusion terms.
3. Allow the casino to restrict or close access.
4. Seek independent support where appropriate.

Independent support comes from GamCare, Gamblers Anonymous and Gambling Therapy. Self-assessment questions help players identify possible gambling-related harm, which makes them a practical starting point for deciding whether to ask for a break.
