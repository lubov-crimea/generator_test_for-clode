Longfu88 sets up every player account around two items, an e-mail plus the password picked during registration. That same pair is what a Longfu88 login asks for later, and no other credential is given. Playing is for adults of 18 years or more, while this page is directed at readers in Singapore. Following the order of the account, the sections cover signing up, safeguarding the data, account rules, time away, settings and help with questions.
