To stop playing, a customer of Longfu88 turns to customer support and requests either self-exclusion or the closing of the account. Under self-exclusion, login and play may be restricted for an agreed time, whereas closure goes through the same support route. One request therefore opens both paths, and the choice between a restriction and an ended account is made inside it. Playing is meant for adults of 18 and older, and only where the law allows. The request follows four steps:

1. Approach support with a wish for self-exclusion or for the account to be closed.
2. Verify the account details and state the exclusion terms the player prefers.
3. Let the casino apply the restriction or close the access.
4. Turn to independent support when appropriate.

Beyond the casino, independent help is offered by GamCare, and also by Gambling Therapy and Gamblers Anonymous. Self-assessment questions let a player notice signs of harm linked to gambling, so they work as a sensible first move when deciding on a break.
