Customer support answers account, login and verification questions through a 24/7 live chat and by e-mail at support@longfu88.com. Typical matters are a failed login, a reset e-mail that does not arrive and the upload of documents for verification. Answer times are not promised for either channel. The support team works in English, Malay, Simplified Chinese, Thai, Indonesian and Vietnamese, so a player may describe the problem in the language that is easiest to write.
