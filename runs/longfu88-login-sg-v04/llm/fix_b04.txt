At Longfu88, a player uses one personal account in one of the supported currencies: EUR, USD, MYR, THB, SGD or USDT. The rules attached to that account are short. A player must register with accurate information, use only one personal account and avoid third-party payment methods. Each rule concerns the player's own conduct: truthful details at registration, a single account for one person and payments made with methods that belong to that person. Residents must also be legally permitted to gamble in their place of residence, a condition that rests with the player.
