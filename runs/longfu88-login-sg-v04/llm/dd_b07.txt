Questions about an account, a login or verification are answered by customer support, via round-the-clock live chat or an e-mail to support@longfu88.com. Failed logins, reset e-mails that never show up and document uploads for verification are the usual topics. No answer times are promised on either channel. Staff handle Malay, Vietnamese, English, Thai, Simplified Chinese and Indonesian, so the problem can be described in the player's most comfortable language among those.
