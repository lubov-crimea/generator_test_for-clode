Accounts at Longfu88 are held one per player, in a single currency chosen from SGD, USDT, THB, USD, MYR and EUR. Three requirements apply to the player: truthful registration data, one personal account only, and no use of payment methods that belong to someone else. All three describe the player's own conduct, since the details given must be true, one person holds one account and money moves only through methods in that person's name. Players must in addition have the legal right to gamble where they live. That condition is theirs to meet.
