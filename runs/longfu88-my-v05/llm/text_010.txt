Verification is required before a withdrawal, and it can also start with unusual or high-value transactions or with changes to account or payment details. The documents requested fall into four groups:

- Identity: passport, national identity card or driving licence
- Address: utility bill or bank statement
- Proof of ownership of the payment method
- Source of funds, when requested

Documents can be sent within 30 days of a request, and a complete set is normally reviewed within 10 days. Withdrawals may be held until the checks are done, while failed checks can mean refused transactions or account restrictions.
