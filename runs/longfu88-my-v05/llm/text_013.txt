Customer support has two channels: live chat, which is available 24/7, and e-mail at support@longfu88.com. Live chat suits a quick question during a session, while e-mail fits a request that needs a written record, such as a query about verification or a bonus. Round-the-clock chat means a late-evening problem does not wait for the morning. Support speaks six languages, namely English, Malay, Simplified Chinese, Thai, Indonesian and Vietnamese, so a player from Malaysia can write in Malay or English.
