The moment a withdrawal is requested is when the account and its payment details get examined. Longfu88 may keep a payment on hold until identification, ownership of the payment method and any needed source-of-funds checks are finished, since such identity and payment checks can be required before withdrawals or promotional proceeds become available. For the player, it makes sense to prepare the documents from the previous section before the first payout is asked for, not afterwards.

As a general rule, a withdrawal request is processed within three working days, an indication rather than a promise of speed. A single rule covers every request: the payment method has to be the player's own, and no third-party method may be used. The checks carry real consequences when they are failed: a transaction may be refused, the account may be restricted, or withdrawals may be withheld until compliance is sorted out.
