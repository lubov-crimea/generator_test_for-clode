Customer support answers account, login and verification questions through live chat, which is open 24/7, and by e-mail at support@longfu88.com. Typical cases are a login that fails despite correct details, a reset e-mail that does not arrive and a problem with a document upload. The support team works in English, Malay, Simplified Chinese, Thai, Indonesian and Vietnamese, so a player can describe the problem in a familiar language. Live chat suits a question that needs an answer during the same session, while an e-mail fits a case with several documents or details attached.
