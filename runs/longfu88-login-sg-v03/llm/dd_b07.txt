Live chat (24/7) and the address support@longfu88.com are the two ways to reach customer support, which deals with questions on the account, the login and verification. Cases that arrive most often include a login failing even though the details are right, a reset message that never turns up and trouble with uploading a document. Messages can be written in English, Malay, Thai, Indonesian, Vietnamese or Simplified Chinese, whichever the player finds easiest. Support is also where a player turns when the registered mailbox cannot be opened anymore and so no reset link can be received.
