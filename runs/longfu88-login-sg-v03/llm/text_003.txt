Most failed logins come down to a small mismatch. The e-mail address has to be typed exactly as registered, and a wrong keyboard layout or an active caps lock can turn a correct password into a wrong one. When the password is lost, a reset link requested with the registered e-mail is the way back in. A player without an account has nothing to sign in to, so the route starts with the Longfu88 register step. The account holder at Longfu88 is also responsible for safeguarding credentials, which is one more reason to keep them private.

Some failures are not typing errors at all. The operator may suspend activity for verification or suspected abuse, and prolonged inactivity may trigger an account review or closure under the applicable terms. An account review may delay access until verification is complete. In those cases, live chat (available 24/7) and e-mail are the places to ask what is holding the account.
