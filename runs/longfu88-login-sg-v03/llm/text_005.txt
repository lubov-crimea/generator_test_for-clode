A withdrawal request is the point where the account and its payment details come under inspection. Identity and payment checks may be required for access to withdrawals or promotional proceeds, so Longfu88 can hold a payment until identification, payment ownership and any required source-of-funds checks are complete. For the player, this means the paperwork described above is worth preparing before the first payout is requested, not after it.

Withdrawal requests are generally processed within three working days, which is a general timeframe and not a promise of speed. One rule applies to every request: the payment method must belong to the player, and third-party payment methods must not be used. Failing the checks has concrete consequences. It can lead to a refused transaction, account restrictions or the withholding of withdrawals until compliance is settled.
