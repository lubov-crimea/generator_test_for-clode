Two details open an account at Longfu88: the address given at sign-up and a password set at the same time, both entered afresh each time a returning player comes back. The audience here is Singapore, and admission is for adults of 18 and over. What follows traces a failed sign-in from start to finish: the standard route in, recovery of a forgotten password, the usual causes of a broken login, account verification, the checks ahead of a payout and the support channels that cover each case.
