A small mismatch lies behind most login failures. The e-mail must match the registration character for character, and a different keyboard layout or caps lock left on is enough to make the right password look wrong. For a forgotten password the remedy is fixed: ask for a reset link by e-mail. Anyone who has not yet opened an account has nothing to sign in to, so the path begins with the Longfu88 register step. At Longfu88 the account holder also answers for safeguarding the credentials.

Not every failure is a typing error. The operator can suspend activity while it checks identity or investigates suspected abuse, and long inactivity may end in the account being reviewed or closed according to the terms in force. During a review, access may wait until verification finishes. A question put to live chat (24/7), or sent by e-mail, shows what is holding the account up.
