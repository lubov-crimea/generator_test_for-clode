Verification at Longfu88 is requested on specific triggers: during an account review, before a withdrawal, for unusual or high-value transactions and after changes to account or payment details. The documents that count fall into four groups, and the last group applies only when it is requested.

- Identity: passport, national identity card or driving licence.
- Address: utility bill or bank statement.
- Payment method: bank or card ownership evidence, or digital wallet ownership evidence.
- Source of funds: proof of income, bank statements or other supporting financial documents.

Time matters here. Documents are due up to 30 days after the request, and a complete set is normally reviewed within up to 10 days. The procedure itself is short: the player opens the verification request in the account or follows the support instructions, uploads legible, current documents that match the account profile, completes telephone or face verification if requested, and waits for approval before a withdrawal.
