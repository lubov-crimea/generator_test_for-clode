Registration at Longfu88 is by e-mail only, and the form asks for an e-mail address, a password, the legal name, the date of birth and the residential address. The player account then opens on a phone the same way as on a computer: the player selects Login on the website and enters the registered e-mail and password. Anyone who forgot the password uses Forgot Password on the login screen, which a separate page of the site describes in detail.
