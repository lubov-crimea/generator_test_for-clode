The casino works in the browser of a smartphone or tablet, and Longfu88 can also be kept as a home-screen shortcut. The next two sections give the steps for Android in Chrome and for iOS in Safari.

=== b04 ===
On an Android phone, Chrome adds a shortcut of the casino website to the home screen through its browser menu, in three steps.

1. Open the casino website in Chrome.
2. Tap the browser menu and choose Add to Home screen.
3. Confirm the shortcut.

The shortcut then appears among the apps of the phone.

=== b05 ===
On iOS, Safari adds a shortcut of the casino website to the home screen of an iPhone or iPad, again in three steps.

1. Open the casino website in Safari.
2. Tap Share and choose Add to Home Screen.
3. Confirm Add.

The new icon opens the casino website like any other icon on the home screen.
