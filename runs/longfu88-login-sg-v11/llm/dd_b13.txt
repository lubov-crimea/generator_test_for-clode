Two routes exist for a player who wants to limit or end play at Longfu88, and both begin with customer support: a self-exclusion request or an account closure request. When self-exclusion is in place, gambling and access to the account can be restricted for the period agreed. Gambling requires an age of 18 and is allowed only where the law permits it. Questions for self-assessment help a player to recognise possible gambling-related harm before deciding on the fitting route. Independent help comes from three organisations that work separately from the casino: Gambling Therapy, GamCare and Gamblers Anonymous.
