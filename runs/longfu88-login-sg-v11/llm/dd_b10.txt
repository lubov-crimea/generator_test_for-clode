In practice the account gets checked at the moment of withdrawal, since access to withdrawals can hinge on checks of identity and payments. A payment can stay on hold until identification, source-of-funds checks where required and confirmation that the payment method is the player's own have been completed, so the checks precede the money rather than follow it. As a rule, a withdrawal request gets processed within three working days. At Longfu88 the payment method used must also belong to the player, since the terms do not allow third-party payment methods.
