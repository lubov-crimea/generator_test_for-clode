Account security is split between the player and the operator, and the player's share comes first. The account holder is responsible for safeguarding the login credentials, the e-mail address and the password, since nothing else opens the account. Longfu88 applies technical and organisational safeguards that protect accounts, payments and personal information. Activity on an account can also be suspended while verification is pending or abuse is suspected, which is why the document checks in the compliance section matter.
