Responsibility for account security is divided, and the player's part comes first. The account holder must keep the login details private, which means the e-mail address along with its password, as nothing else unlocks the account. On the operator side, Longfu88 applies safeguards of a technical and organisational kind, covering personal information, payments and accounts. Freezing is also possible when abuse is suspected or verification has not finished, which explains the weight of the document checks in the compliance section.
