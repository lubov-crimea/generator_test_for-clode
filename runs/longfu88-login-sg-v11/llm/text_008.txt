A player who wants to limit or end play at Longfu88 has two routes, and both start with customer support: a request for self-exclusion or a request for account closure. Once self-exclusion is in place, account access and gambling may be restricted for the agreed period. Players must be at least 18 and gamble only where lawful, and self-assessment questions help a player identify possible gambling-related harm before deciding which route fits. Independent help is available from GamCare, Gamblers Anonymous and Gambling Therapy, organisations that work separately from the casino.
