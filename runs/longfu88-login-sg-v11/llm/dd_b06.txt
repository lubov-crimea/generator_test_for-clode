The e-mail has to match the registered spelling exactly. What the password field receives also depends on the keyboard layout and on whether caps lock is on. Asking for a reset link with the registered e-mail solves a lost password. Another cause is a block that Longfu88 can set on an account for verification purposes or in case of suspected abuse, and reviews of that kind may delay access until verification finishes. Support answers such cases by e-mail, and through live chat that works around the clock, 24/7.
