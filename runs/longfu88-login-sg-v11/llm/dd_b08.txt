Three groups of personal data are held about an account holder, answering who the player is, how to reach the player and whether the payment methods are the player's own:

- Identity data: name, identity documents and birth date.
- Contact data: e-mail, telephone and residential address.
- Verification data: KYC documents, with payment method ownership evidence.

Longfu88 may pass the data on to technology providers, identity verification providers and payment service providers, and, when required, to law enforcement and regulators. The player's rights: erasure where legally permitted, access, rectification, restriction, objection, withdrawal of consent and, where applicable, portability.
