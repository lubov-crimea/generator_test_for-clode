Gambling here is meant for those aged 18 and above, wherever local law lets them play. To self-exclude, a player writes to support@longfu88.com. After processing, the block on account access and gambling, set for the agreed period, begins inside 24 hours. Customer support also handles account closure, which proceeds through four steps: contact, agreeing the exclusion terms with the account details, the casino restricting or closing access, and outside help where suitable. Self-assessment questions that players can put to themselves include whether stakes exceed what was planned, whether losses are being chased and whether play is kept from family. Three organisations offer help:

- Gambling Therapy
- GamCare
- Gamblers Anonymous

This page cannot replace that independent support.
