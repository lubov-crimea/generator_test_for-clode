Mobile play starts in the browser: the casino runs as a responsive website on smartphone and tablet browsers, across Android, iOS and mobile web. Adding it takes a few taps, and the Android route comes first:

1. Open Longfu88 in Chrome.
2. Open browser menu and choose Add to Home screen.
3. Confirm the shortcut.

On iOS the route runs through Safari instead:

1. Open Longfu88 in Safari.
2. Tap Share and choose Add to Home Screen.
