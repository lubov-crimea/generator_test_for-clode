Responsible play starts with the age limit: players must be at least 18 years old and gamble only where it is lawful. Self-exclusion is requested by e-mail to customer support at support@longfu88.com, takes effect within 24 hours after the request is processed and may restrict account access and gambling for the agreed period. Account closure is requested through customer support as well. The procedure is short: contact support, confirm the account details and exclusion terms, allow the casino to restrict or close access and seek independent support where appropriate.

Self-assessment questions help players identify possible gambling-related harm, and three organisations offer independent help, which this page cannot replace:

- GamCare
- Gamblers Anonymous
- Gambling Therapy
