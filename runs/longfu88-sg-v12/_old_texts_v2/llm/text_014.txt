Customer support works through two channels: live chat, which is available 24/7, and e-mail at support@longfu88.com. Live chat suits quick questions about a deposit or a bonus, while e-mail fits requests that need a written record, such as self-exclusion or account closure. Support speaks English, Malay, Simplified Chinese, Thai, Indonesian and Vietnamese, so players can write in the language they use most comfortably. For anything urgent about a payment, live chat is the channel to try first.
