KYC verification may be required before a withdrawal, and Longfu88 can also ask for it during an account review, on unusual or high-value transactions and after changes to account or payment details. The accepted documents are:

- Identity: passport, national identity card or driving licence.
- Address: utility bill or bank statement.
- Payment method: evidence of bank, card or digital wallet ownership.
- Source of funds, when requested: proof of income, bank statements or other supporting financial documents.

Documents can be sent up to 30 days after the request and must be legible, current and consistent with the account profile. A complete set is normally reviewed within 10 days of receipt, and telephone or face verification may be added. Withdrawals may be held until the checks are done, and failed checks can lead to refused transactions or account restrictions.
