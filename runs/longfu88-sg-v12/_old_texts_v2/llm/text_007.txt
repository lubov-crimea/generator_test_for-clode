The Longfu88 VIP Program credits eligible real-money play with VIP points, and these points unlock numbered reward levels. Its 25 levels are grouped into five tiers: Bronze, Silver, Gold, Platinum and Diamond. Level 1 needs 1 point and carries a level reward of €9.8, cashback of 1% and a withdrawal limit of €9,800. Level 25 needs 5,500 points and carries €980, cashback of 10% and a withdrawal limit of €147,000. Higher levels raise the withdrawal limits and the cashback and add personalised offers, while a personal VIP manager is available from level 4. Rewards and withdrawal limits may differ by VIP level and currency, and the level-based benefits and promotion eligibility may change under the programme rules.
