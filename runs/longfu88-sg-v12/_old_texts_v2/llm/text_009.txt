Registration at Longfu88 is done by email and requires an email address, a password, the legal name, the date of birth and the residential address, and a player may hold only one personal account. The five steps run in this order:

1. Open the website and select Register.
2. Enter email and create a password.
3. Confirm age and acceptance of Terms and Conditions and Privacy Policy.
4. Complete the additional personal details and residential address.
5. Select Create Account and complete any requested email confirmation.

The Longfu88 login works with the registered email and password: select Login on the website, enter both and complete any security check to reach the account. A forgotten password is recovered on the same screen. Choose Forgot Password, request a reset link to the registered email and follow the emailed instructions to set a new password.
