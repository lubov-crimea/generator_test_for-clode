The casino runs as a responsive website on smartphone and tablet browsers, and its listed platforms are Android, iOS and mobile web. A shortcut on the home screen gives quick access to the casino and sportsbook, and this home-screen version of the site is what the Longfu88 app means here. Casino, sportsbook, account access, deposits, withdrawals and bonuses are all available on mobile.

On Android the shortcut is added through Chrome in three steps:

1. Open Longfu88 in Chrome.
2. Open browser menu and choose Add to Home screen.
3. Confirm the shortcut.

On iOS the same shortcut is added through Safari:

1. Open Longfu88 in Safari.
2. Tap Share and choose Add to Home Screen.
3. Confirm Add.
