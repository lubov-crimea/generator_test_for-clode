Players can reach customer support in two ways: through live chat and by e-mail at support@longfu88.com. Live chat stays open 24/7, which suits short questions on payments or promotions at any hour. The e-mail route fits longer requests, such as those tied to verification or account closure. Support speaks six languages: Thai, Malay, English, Indonesian, Vietnamese and Simplified Chinese. The choice between the two channels depends mainly on how urgent the matter is.
