A player account at Longfu88 is created by e-mail registration. The form asks for an e-mail address, a password, the legal name, the date of birth and the residential address. Once the account exists, signing in from a phone works exactly as it does on a computer: the player selects Login on the website, enters the registered e-mail and password, completes any security check and reaches the account. Anyone who has forgotten the password uses Forgot Password on the login screen.
