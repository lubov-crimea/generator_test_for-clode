- **Is there a store app to download?** No, Longfu88 opens in the mobile browser and is added to the home screen as a shortcut.
- **How does a player log in on a phone?** With the registered e-mail and password, as on a computer.
- **What is the minimum age?** It is 18, and the player must be legally permitted to gamble where they live.
- **How is the iOS shortcut added?** In Safari, through Share and Add to Home Screen, then Add.
- **How is the Android shortcut added?** In Chrome, through the browser menu and Add to Home screen, then the confirmation.
- **Where can a player turn when something fails?** To Longfu88 customer support, by live chat 24/7 or by e-mail.
