Two channels handle player questions. Live chat stays reachable around the clock, so a question raised after midnight does not have to wait, while longer, detailed queries fit the mailbox at support@longfu88.com. Customer support replies in six languages: Simplified Chinese, English, Malay, Vietnamese, Thai and Indonesian. Questions about bonuses, payouts or account checks can be raised through either route, so the choice mostly comes down to speed versus detail for the player.
