Slots and live dealer games head the list of nine categories at Longfu88, and each has its own sub-section below with examples and studios. Table games, jackpots and fishing complete the traditional side of the lobby, while P2P games, fast games, lottery, and crash and instant games add quicker or more specialised formats to the lobby. Crash and instant games are quick-round formats, so they differ from slots mainly in pace, and a player who prefers longer sessions will find the slot and table categories closer to that style of play.
