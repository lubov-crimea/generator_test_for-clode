The casino may ask for account verification before a withdrawal, during an account review and for unusual or high-value transactions.

| Type | Accepted documents |
|---|---|
| Identity | Passport, national identity card, driving licence |
| Address | Utility bill, bank statement |
| Payment method | Bank or card ownership evidence, digital wallet ownership evidence |
| Source of funds (when requested) | Proof of income, bank statements, other supporting financial documents |

Review of a complete document set normally takes up to 10 days.
