Customer support works through two channels: a live chat that is available 24/7 and e-mail at support@longfu88.com. Because the chat never closes, it suits questions that come up outside office hours, while e-mail is the natural route for longer requests such as self-exclusion or account closure and leaves a written record. Customer support speaks English, Malay, Simplified Chinese, Thai, Indonesian and Vietnamese, so a player in Singapore can choose English, Simplified Chinese or Malay when getting in touch.
