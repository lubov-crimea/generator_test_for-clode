Players must be at least 18 years old and gamble only where it is lawful. Self-exclusion is requested by e-mail to customer support at support@longfu88.com, and it restricts account access and gambling for the agreed period, within 24 hours after the request is processed. Account closure is requested through customer support as well. In both cases the procedure is short: contact support, confirm the account details and the preferred exclusion terms, allow the casino to restrict or close access, and seek independent support where appropriate. Self-assessment questions help players identify possible gambling-related harm before it grows.

Three independent organisations offer help:

- GamCare
- Gamblers Anonymous
- Gambling Therapy

This page cannot replace independent help, so a player who recognises signs of harm should contact one of them.
