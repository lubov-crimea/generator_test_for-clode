Mobile access works through a responsive website that adapts to smartphone and tablet browsers on Android, iOS and mobile web. Adding the site to the home screen creates a shortcut, and the Longfu88 app is this home-screen version of the site. The shortcut gives quick access to the casino and sportsbook, as well as account access, deposits, withdrawals and bonuses. Each route takes three steps.

On Android, the shortcut is added in Chrome:

1. Open Longfu88 in Chrome.
2. Open browser menu and choose Add to Home screen.
3. Confirm the shortcut.

On iOS, the route runs through Safari:

1. Open Longfu88 in Safari.
2. Tap Share and choose Add to Home Screen.
3. Confirm Add.
