The mobile website includes a sportsbook, and its coverage spans nine sports:

- Basketball
- Esports
- Cricket
- Tennis
- Table tennis
- Football
- Virtual football
- Volleyball
- Badminton

Single and accumulator bets sit alongside handicap, totals, outrights, live and Bet Builder wagers. Live betting adds odds that update live and markets open in-play, so a bet can still be placed once an event has started. Availability of events and markets can differ by jurisdiction.
