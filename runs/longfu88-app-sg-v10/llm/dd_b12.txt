Cashing out begins in the cashier on the phone site, and six steps follow the login:

1. Sign in, then go to the cashier.
2. In Withdrawal, select a supported payment method as the destination.
3. Specify the amount and the receiving wallet, bank or blockchain address.
4. Identity verification and payment-method verification follow when requested.
5. Review the details, then submit.
6. The player gets the money once the casino has finished with the request and any bank or network involved has settled it.

Requests normally finish within three working days, though bank transfers can take a further 3–5 business days to settle. KYC documents can be requested by the operator before processing.
