The sportsbook is one of the features of the mobile website and covers nine sports:

- Football
- Basketball
- Tennis
- Badminton
- Cricket
- Table tennis
- Volleyball
- Esports
- Virtual football

The bet types are single, accumulator, live, handicap, totals, outrights and Bet Builder. Live betting offers live odds and in-play markets, so a bet can be placed while an event is running. Event availability and market selection can vary by jurisdiction.
