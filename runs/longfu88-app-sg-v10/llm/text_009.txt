The cashier is part of the mobile website, so deposits are made in the same phone browser that opens the casino. Singapore players have four methods in Singapore dollars: two PayNow routes and two bank options. All four show SGD as the currency and the same deposit speed.

| Method | Currency | Deposit speed |
|---|---|---|
| PayNow | SGD | 1–2 minutes |
| PayNow QR | SGD | 1–2 minutes |
| DBS Bank | SGD | 1–2 minutes |
| OCBC Bank | SGD | 1–2 minutes |

A player who pays with PayNow, PayNow QR, DBS Bank or OCBC Bank therefore waits the same time whichever of them is chosen.
