Opening Longfu88 on a phone starts in the browser of a smartphone or tablet. A home-screen shortcut is added once, and from then on it sits among the other icons. Nobody needs an installation file or a Longfu88 apk, because the casino loads as a website and the shortcut is not a store listing. The next two sections give the steps for Android in Chrome and for iOS in Safari.

=== b03 ===
On Android phones, Chrome adds a shortcut of the casino website to the home screen in three steps:

1. Open the casino website in Chrome.
2. Open the browser menu and choose Add to Home screen.
3. Confirm the shortcut.

The shortcut then appears among the apps of the phone.

=== b04 ===
On iOS, Safari puts a shortcut of the casino website on the iPhone or iPad home screen through the Share menu:

1. Open the casino website in Safari.
2. Tap Share and choose Add to Home Screen.
3. Confirm Add.

The icon then sits beside the other items on the home screen.
