The minimum age is 18, and local law decides where gambling is allowed at all. A player who chooses self-exclusion e-mails customer support at support@longfu88.com, and that message is what starts it. After the request is processed, limits on account access and play apply within 24 hours for the period agreed. An account can be closed through customer support too, in four steps: the player states the preferred terms, confirms the account details, accepts that access may be restricted or closed, and looks for independent support in suitable cases. Self-assessment questions let players check for signs of harm. Gambling Therapy, Gamblers Anonymous and GamCare are the organisations players are pointed toward, and a page like this is no substitute for them.
