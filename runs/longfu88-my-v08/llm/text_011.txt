In the VIP programme, points come from eligible real-money play, and VIP points unlock numbered reward levels. The 25 levels are grouped into the tiers Bronze, Silver, Gold, Platinum and Diamond.

Level 1 needs 1 point and carries a level reward of €9.8, 1% cashback and a withdrawal limit of €9,800. At the top, level 25 needs 5,500 points and carries €980, 10% cashback and a withdrawal limit of €147,000. Higher levels raise withdrawal limits and cashback and add personalised offers, and a personal VIP manager is available from level 4.

Reward and withdrawal limits may differ by VIP level and currency, and the rules can change, so check the current terms before aiming for a level.
