Four checks cover the common phone problems at Longfu88, and support helps with the problems that these checks do not solve.

| Problem | Fix |
|---|---|
| The shortcut does not appear | Repeat the steps in Chrome on Android or in Safari on iOS |
| The page does not load | Reload the page and check the internet connection |
| The login fails | Check the e-mail address and use Forgot Password on the login screen |
| The problem remains | Contact support through live chat, 24/7, or by e-mail, and describe the step that failed |
