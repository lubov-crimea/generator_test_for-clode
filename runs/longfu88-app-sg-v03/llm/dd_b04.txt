E-mail is the sole registration route at Longfu88, and the rest of the form asks for a password, legal name, birth date and residential address. A phone login mirrors the desktop one: e-mail plus password, in three steps that begin at the Login page.

- Tap Login on the site.
- Type the account e-mail and its password.
- Land inside the account.

A lost password is handled by the Forgot Password option found on the login screen.
