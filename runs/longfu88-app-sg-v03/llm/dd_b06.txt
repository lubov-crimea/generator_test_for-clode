The mobile website hosts the cashier, which means deposits start from the browser of the phone or tablet. Four methods are on offer: DBS Bank, PayNow, OCBC Bank and PayNow QR, all processed in Singapore dollars.

| Method | Processing time | Currency |
|---|---|---|
| DBS Bank | 1 to 2 minutes | Singapore dollars (SGD) |
| PayNow | 1 to 2 minutes | Singapore dollars (SGD) |
| OCBC Bank | 1 to 2 minutes | Singapore dollars (SGD) |
| PayNow QR | 1 to 2 minutes | Singapore dollars (SGD) |

Because currency and deposit speed are identical for all four, the choice depends on the bank or QR route that the player already uses.
