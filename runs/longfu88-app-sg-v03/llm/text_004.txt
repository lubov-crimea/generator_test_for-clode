Registration at Longfu88 is by e-mail only, and the form asks for e-mail, password, legal name, date of birth and residential address. Login works on a phone as on a computer: the method is e-mail and password, in three steps from the login page.

- Select Login on the website.
- Enter the registered e-mail and password.
- Access the account.

A player who forgot the password chooses Forgot Password on the login screen.
