The VIP programme rewards continued play from a logged-in account and has five tiers: Bronze, Silver, Gold, Platinum and Diamond. A player earns VIP points through eligible real-money gameplay, and the points unlock numbered reward levels. Additional benefits include personalised offers, VIP cashback, higher withdrawal limits and a personal VIP manager for eligible high tiers. Different reward and withdrawal limits may apply to VIP levels and currencies, and level-based benefits and promotion eligibility may change under the programme rules, so the current terms are worth checking.
