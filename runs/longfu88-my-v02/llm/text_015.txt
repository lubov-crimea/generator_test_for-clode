Customer support works through two channels: a live chat that is available 24/7 and e-mail at support@longfu88.com. Live chat suits quick questions about a deposit, a bonus activation or a verification request, while e-mail is the channel for longer cases such as the self-exclusion and account closure requests described earlier. Support is offered in English, Malay, Simplified Chinese, Thai, Indonesian and Vietnamese, so players from Malaysia can write in Malay or English. Having the account email and the relevant transaction details at hand makes any request easier to answer.
