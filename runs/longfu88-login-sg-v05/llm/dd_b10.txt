The support team looks after account matters, from login to verification. Live chat is staffed 24/7, and the mailbox is support@longfu88.com. Typical matters are trouble uploading a document, a reset message that never reaches the inbox and a sign-in that fails. Replies are available in Malay, English, Thai, Indonesian, Simplified Chinese and Vietnamese, so players in Singapore may write in whichever of these languages is the most comfortable.
