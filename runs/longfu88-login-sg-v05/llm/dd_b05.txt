Recovery of a lost password begins on the login screen and ends with a link delivered by e-mail. Three steps cover it.

1. Click Forgot Password, found on the sign-in screen.
2. Order a reset link by giving the address that was registered.
3. Open the message that arrives and carry out its instructions, which end in a new password.

Without access to that inbox, the matter goes to customer support, whose channels appear in the help section below.
