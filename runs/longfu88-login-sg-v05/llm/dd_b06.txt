Small typing slips cause most failed logins, so the first checks are easy ones. Spelling of the e-mail should match the registration letter for letter, caps lock should be off with the right keyboard layout active, and a forgotten password calls for the reset described earlier. If none of this helps, the reason may sit with the account itself. Longfu88 has the right to freeze activity while documents are checked, or when misuse is suspected, and the player may have to wait for entry until that review ends. Such a case belongs with support, which answers through the 24/7 chat window and also takes e-mail.
