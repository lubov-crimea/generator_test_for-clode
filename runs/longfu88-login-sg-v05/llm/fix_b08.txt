Account security starts with the holder: the account holder is responsible for safeguarding the login credentials, which here means the registered e-mail address and the password. The operator has a part as well. Longfu88 applies technical and organizational safeguards that protect accounts, payments and personal information, and it may suspend activity for verification or suspected abuse. Because the login rests on e-mail and password alone, a player who keeps the password private and uses the reset when it is lost looks after the part of security that stays in the player's own hands.
