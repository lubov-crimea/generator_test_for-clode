Responsibility for the login details lies first with the account holder, who has to keep the registered e-mail address and its password safe. The operator carries a share of the work too. Longfu88 uses technical and organizational measures to guard personal data, payments and accounts. It can also suspend activity when verification is due or abuse is suspected. As the login consists of nothing beyond e-mail and password, a player who keeps the password private and resets it after losing it covers the part of security that depends on the player.
