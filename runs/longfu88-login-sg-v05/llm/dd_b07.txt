Verification means that the casino confirms, through submitted documents, that the account belongs to the person who registered it. Longfu88 does not require this at sign-up or at login. A request is made in four situations: when the account comes under review, ahead of any withdrawal, when transfers of money look unusual or very large, and if account or payment information is changed. Proof of identity, for instance a passport, comes first, and the table lists the other kinds. Documents on the source of funds are needed solely if the casino asks for them.

| Category | Documents that qualify |
|---|---|
| Who the player is | Passport, driving licence, or the identity card issued nationally |
| Where the player lives | Either a utility bill or else a statement issued by the bank |
| Payment method | Ownership proof for the bank account or card used, or for a digital wallet |
| Origin of funds | Proof of income, statements from banks and any other supporting financial papers |

After a request, the documents are due within 30 days at most. A complete submission is normally processed within 10 days at the latest.
