Longfu88 is a responsive website, so mobile access needs only the browser of a smartphone or a tablet, and the login there is the same as on a computer. A shortcut on the home screen then puts the site one tap away. Android and iOS each set it up in three moves.

- Android: open the site in Chrome, open the browser menu and choose Add to Home screen, then confirm the shortcut.
- iOS: open the site in Safari, tap Share and choose Add to Home Screen, then confirm Add.
