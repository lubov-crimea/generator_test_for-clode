The first withdrawal is the point where an account gets checked. At Longfu88, identity and payment checks may be required for access to withdrawals or promotional proceeds, and a payment may be held until identification, payment ownership and any required source-of-funds checks are complete. A request therefore waits for those steps, so it helps to have them in order before asking for money to be sent out. Withdrawal requests are generally processed within three working days, which is a general expectation and not a promise of speed. One rule applies to every request: payment methods must belong to the player, and third-party payment methods are not to be used. A card or wallet in someone else's name cannot serve as the route for a payout.
