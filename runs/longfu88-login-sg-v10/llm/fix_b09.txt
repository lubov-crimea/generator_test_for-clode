Players at Longfu88 must be at least 18 years old and gamble only where it is lawful. With self-exclusion, account access and gambling may be restricted for the agreed period, so the restriction applies to the time that was set and not beyond it. Closing the account is also requested through customer support. Independent organisations offer help outside the casino: GamCare, Gamblers Anonymous and Gambling Therapy. A player can contact any of them whether or not the account has been restricted.
