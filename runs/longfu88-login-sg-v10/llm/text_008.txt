A VIP programme runs alongside the account at Longfu88, with five tiers: Bronze, Silver, Gold, Platinum and Diamond. Progress depends on play. A player earns VIP points through eligible real-money gameplay, and the points unlock numbered reward levels, so only qualifying play moves the account upward. The extra benefits are personalised offers, VIP cashback and higher withdrawal limits, while a personal VIP manager is kept for eligible high tiers. Reward and withdrawal limits may differ by VIP level and currency, and level-based benefits and eligibility may change under the programme rules, so the current terms are worth checking before a player counts on a particular benefit.
