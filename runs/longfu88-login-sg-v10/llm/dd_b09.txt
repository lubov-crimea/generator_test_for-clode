Age 18 is the minimum at Longfu88, and gambling is permitted solely where it is lawful to gamble. To take a pause or stop playing for good, a player contacts customer support and asks either for self-exclusion or for account closure. Under self-exclusion, the player may be cut off from the account and from gambling for the period agreed, though not beyond that period. Account closure is arranged through support as well. GamCare is one of three independent organisations offering help outside the casino, the others being Gambling Therapy and Gamblers Anonymous, and anyone can contact them, restricted account or not.
