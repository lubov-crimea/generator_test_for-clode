A first payout request is the moment an account faces real checking. At Longfu88, identity and payment checks may stand in the way of withdrawals and promotional proceeds, and funds may wait while identification, the owner of the payment method and, if demanded, the origin of the money are all confirmed. Because the request waits on this, it helps to have the documents ready beforehand. Processing within three working days is what is generally expected, stated as a norm and not as a promised speed. Each request is subject to one condition: whichever payment method is used must belong to the player, and third-party methods are excluded. Money cannot leave through a card or wallet held in another person's name.
