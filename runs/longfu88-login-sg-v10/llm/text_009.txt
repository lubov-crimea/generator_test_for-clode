Players at Longfu88 must be at least 18 years old and gamble only where it is lawful. A player who wants a pause or an end to play contacts customer support and requests self-exclusion or account closure. With self-exclusion, account access and gambling may be restricted for the agreed period, so the length of the break is settled with support instead of being left to chance. Closing the account is also requested through customer support. Independent organisations offer help outside the casino: GamCare, Gamblers Anonymous and Gambling Therapy. A player can contact any of them whether or not the account has been restricted.
