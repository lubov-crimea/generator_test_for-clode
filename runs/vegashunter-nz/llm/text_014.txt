Gambling is for adults only, and the minimum age at VegasHunter is 18. Five tools support responsible play, and most of them are set through player support. Deposit limits can be requested from support or through account functions, although deposits made via cryptoprocessing cannot be limited. A temporary cooling-off period of 24 hours, 7 days or 30 days may be arranged, and self-exclusion is available on request, subject to verification and an agreed exclusion period. Players who want to stop altogether can ask support to close the account for gambling-control reasons. Session awareness completes the set, since players are encouraged to monitor their time and spending daily. For outside help, GamCare, Gambling Therapy and Gamblers Anonymous offer external support.
