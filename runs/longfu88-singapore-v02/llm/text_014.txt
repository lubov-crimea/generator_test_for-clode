Mobile play runs through a responsive website that works on smartphone and tablet browsers across Android, iOS and mobile web. The Longfu88 app is the home-screen version of that site: a shortcut that opens the same pages and gives quick access to the casino, the sportsbook, account access, deposits, withdrawals and bonuses. Setting it up takes three steps on either platform.

On Android, the route starts in Chrome:

1. Open Longfu88 in Chrome.
2. Open browser menu and choose Add to Home screen.
3. Confirm the shortcut.

On iOS, the route runs through Safari:

1. Open Longfu88 in Safari.
2. Tap Share and choose Add to Home Screen.
3. Confirm Add.
