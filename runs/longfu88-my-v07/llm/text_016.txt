Players must be at least 18 and may gamble only where it is lawful. Self-exclusion is requested by email to customer support and restricts account access and gambling for the agreed period, with processing within 24 hours after the request is processed. Account closure is also requested through customer support. Both follow a four-step procedure: contact support, confirm the account details and preferred exclusion terms, allow the casino to restrict or close access, and seek independent support where appropriate.

Self-assessment questions help players spot possible gambling-related harm. GamCare, Gamblers Anonymous and Gambling Therapy offer independent help, which this page cannot replace.
