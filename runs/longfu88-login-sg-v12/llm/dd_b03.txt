Longfu88 asks for proof of identity only when something triggers it: a withdrawal, an account review, a transaction out of the ordinary in size or kind, or an update to the profile or the payment details. Four groups of documents are used:

- Identity: a national identity card is accepted, and so are a driving licence and a passport.
- Address: a bank statement stands in for a utility bill, and either will do.
- Payment method: documents showing that the player owns the bank account, card or digital wallet used.
- Source of funds: income proof, bank statements or other financial papers, on request only.

Up to 30 days are given for sending the documents from the date of the request, while a full set normally needs up to 10 days for review. Four steps follow:

1. Find the request in the account, or follow the instructions from support.
2. Upload readable, recent documents that fit the account profile.
3. Complete a telephone or face verification too, if asked.
4. Wait for approval before attempting a withdrawal or anything else restricted.

Deposits, withdrawals and ongoing account monitoring can all trigger a check. A failed one can mean a refused transaction, a restricted account or withdrawals held pending compliance. Each check is thus tied to an event.
