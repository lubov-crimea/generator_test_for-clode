Stopping is the final stage of an account's life. The age minimum is 18, and play is meant only where gambling is lawful. A player who needs time away or wants the account ended turns to customer support, where the request is either self-exclusion or closing the account, and Longfu88 may then restrict access and gambling for the period agreed. Four steps make up the process:

1. Message support and name the request, whether self-exclusion or closure.
2. Check the account details with support and agree the exclusion terms preferred.
3. Let the casino limit or shut down access.
4. Look for independent help where suitable.

Independent organisations also help: Gamblers Anonymous, along with GamCare and Gambling Therapy. A player can use self-assessment questions to check whether gambling-related harm may be present, and that is a practical first step before contacting support. In short, a single message to support starts the whole process.
