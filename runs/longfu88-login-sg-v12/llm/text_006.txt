The moment a player asks for a payout, the account is looked at more closely. Identity and payment checks may be required for access to withdrawals or promotional proceeds, and payments may be held until identification, payment ownership and any required source-of-funds checks are complete. At Longfu88 a withdrawal therefore waits for these checks before it moves on. After that, withdrawal requests are generally processed within three working days, while bank transfers may need another 3–5 business days for settlement. The operator may also request KYC documents before processing payments. One more rule concerns ownership: payment methods must belong to the player, and third-party payment methods are not to be used. In short, the payout depends on completed checks and on a payment method in the player's own name.
