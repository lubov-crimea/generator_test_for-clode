Trouble signing in typically traces back to ordinary causes. Typos come first: the e-mail must be entered precisely as it was registered, and the keyboard layout and the Caps Lock key deserve a look. Where the password is lost, the reset described above applies. Another possibility is an account review, since Longfu88 is allowed to suspend activity pending verification or over suspected abuse, so access may wait until verification is done. Support is the right contact then, by e-mail or through its round-the-clock live chat (24/7).
