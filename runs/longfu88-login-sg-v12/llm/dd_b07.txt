Guarding the login details is the job of the account holder, because the only things that open the account are the password chosen at registration and the matching e-mail address. Longfu88 adds technical and organisational measures of its own, protecting accounts, payments and personal information. The operator can also place activity on pause wherever abuse is suspected or a verification has not yet been completed, and an account may stay paused until that is resolved. Protection is a joint effort: the player looks after the credentials, the operator runs the safeguards.
