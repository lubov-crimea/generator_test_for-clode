Mobile access works through the browser. Longfu88 is a responsive website that adapts to smartphone and tablet screens, and the login on a phone is the same as on a computer: the registered e-mail and password. A home screen shortcut puts the site among the other icons of the device, and each platform has its own short route:

- Android: open the site in Chrome, open the browser menu, choose Add to Home screen and confirm the shortcut.
- iOS: open the site in Safari, tap Share, choose Add to Home Screen and confirm Add.

Both routes take three steps, and the shortcut leads to the same website as the browser does.
