At this stage Longfu88 asks the player for proof of identity, but only on certain triggers: an account review, a withdrawal, unusual or high-value transactions, and changes to account or payment details. Verification is requested before a withdrawal, during an account review and for unusual or high-value transactions, and the documents fall into four groups:

- Identity: passport, national identity card or driving licence.
- Address: utility bill or bank statement.
- Payment method: bank or card ownership evidence, or digital wallet ownership evidence.
- Source of funds: proof of income, bank statements or other supporting financial documents, only when requested.

Documents are due up to 30 days after the request, and a complete set is normally reviewed within up to 10 days. The procedure has four steps:

1. Open the verification request in the account or follow the support instructions.
2. Upload legible, current documents that match the account profile.
3. Complete additional telephone or face verification if requested.
4. Wait for approval before a withdrawal or other restricted activity.

Checks may happen at deposits, at withdrawals and during ongoing account monitoring. A failed check can lead to a refused transaction, account restrictions or withdrawals held pending compliance. Verification, in short, follows the triggers above.
