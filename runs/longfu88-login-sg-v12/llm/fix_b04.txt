With the account open, Longfu88 offers a single way in: e-mail and password. The Longfu88 login takes place on the login screen, where the player enters the registered e-mail and password, and a Longfu login runs through three steps in order:

1. Select Login on the website.
2. Enter the registered e-mail and password.
3. Complete any security check and access the account.

A security check, where one is requested, comes before access to the account. The details set at registration are all that logging in requires.
