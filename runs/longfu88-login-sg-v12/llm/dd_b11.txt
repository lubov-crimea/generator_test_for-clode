Regular play with real money lifts the account up a ladder. The five tiers of the VIP programme at Longfu88 run from Bronze up through Silver and Gold to Platinum and Diamond. VIP points accrue from eligible play for real money, and they open numbered reward levels. High tiers that qualify get a personal VIP manager, along with raised withdrawal limits, VIP cashback and personalised offers. How big a reward or a withdrawal limit is depends on the currency as well as on VIP level, and benefits based on level and eligibility may be changed by the programme rules. The ladder thus tracks eligible play, and its details can change.
