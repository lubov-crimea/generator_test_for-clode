Losing a password does not end access, because Longfu88 lets a new one be set by e-mail:

1. First pick Forgot Password, an option of the login screen.
2. A reset link addressed to the registered mailbox is requested.
3. Follow the instructions in the e-mail to set a new password.

The link goes to that address, so the mailbox is the key to the process. When it cannot be opened, support is the contact, whose channels are listed lower down.
