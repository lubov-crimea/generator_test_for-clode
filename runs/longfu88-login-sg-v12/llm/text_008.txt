Once the account is in regular use, three rules of the terms start to matter. The first concerns inactivity: prolonged inactivity may trigger an account review or closure under the applicable terms. The second concerns disagreements, since a player who wants a dispute or an account decision reviewed contacts customer support and asks for that review. Last, Longfu88 may modify promotional and contractual terms and publish updates to the site, so the terms are worth rereading from time to time. Together, the three rules cover a quiet account, a disagreement and a revised term.
