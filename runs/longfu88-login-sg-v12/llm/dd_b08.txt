A payout request changes how closely the account is examined. Withdrawals and promotional winnings may stay out of reach until identity and payment checks are passed, and a payment may be held until the player is identified, the method of payment is confirmed to be in the player's name and, if required, the source of funds is confirmed. A Longfu88 withdrawal therefore waits for those steps. As a rule, requests are processed in three working days, and bank transfers settle after another 3–5 business days. Before any payment is processed, KYC documents can additionally be asked for. Another rule is ownership: only the player's own payment methods may be used, and a method of a third party is not allowed. So a payout depends on finished checks and a method held in the player's name.
