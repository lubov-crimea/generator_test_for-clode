Safeguarding the login credentials is the account holder's own task, since the e-mail address and the password chosen at registration are the only details that open the account. Longfu88 adds a layer of its own: technical and organisational safeguards protect accounts, payments and personal information. The operator may also suspend activity for verification or suspected abuse, so an account can be paused until the matter is cleared. Protection is thus shared between the player and the operator.
