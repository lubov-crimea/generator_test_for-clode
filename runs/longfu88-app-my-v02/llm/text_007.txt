Four common phone problems usually have a short fix on the Longfu88 site.

- Shortcut missing: repeat the steps in Chrome on Android or Safari on iOS.
- Page not loading: reload it and check the internet connection.
- Login failing: check the e-mail address and use Forgot Password on the login screen.
- Still stuck: customer support handles what these checks do not solve, through 24/7 live chat or e-mail.
