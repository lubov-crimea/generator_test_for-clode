Four problems come up most often when Longfu88 is opened on a phone, and each has a plain fix to try before support is needed:

- Shortcut missing: repeat the steps in Chrome on Android or in Safari on iOS.
- Page not loading: reload it and check the internet connection.
- Login failing: check the e-mail address, then choose Forgot Password on the login screen and request a reset link with the registered e-mail.
- Still stuck: customer support helps with what these checks do not solve, through 24/7 live chat or by e-mail.
