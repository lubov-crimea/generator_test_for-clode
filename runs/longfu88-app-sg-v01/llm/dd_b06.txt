Four issues show up most often when Longfu88 is used on a phone, and each has a simple remedy that can be tried before contacting support:

- Shortcut absent: go through the steps again, with Chrome for Android or with Safari for iOS.
- Page not opening: check the internet connection first, then reload.
- Failed login: verify the e-mail address, then tap the Forgot Password link and ask for a reset message to be emailed to the address used at registration.
- Problem persists: customer support handles what these checks leave unsolved, reachable by e-mail and by 24/7 live chat.
