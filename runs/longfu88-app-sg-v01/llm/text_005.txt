Registration is by e-mail only, and the form asks for an e-mail address, a password, a legal name, a date of birth and a residential address. Logging in works the same on a phone as on a computer: on Longfu88 the player selects Login on the website, enters the registered e-mail and password, completes any security check and reaches the player account. A player who forgot the password chooses Forgot Password on the login screen.
