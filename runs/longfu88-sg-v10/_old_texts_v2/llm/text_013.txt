Mobile play uses a responsive website on smartphone and tablet browsers, with platforms for Android, iOS and mobile web, and it can be added to the home screen as a shortcut. The Longfu88 app is the home-screen version of the site: it opens in one tap and gives quick access to the casino and sportsbook, and the account functions of deposits, withdrawals and bonuses are available on it as well. On Android the shortcut is created in Chrome:

1. Open Longfu88 in Chrome.
2. Open browser menu and choose Add to Home screen.
3. Confirm the shortcut.

On iOS the same result comes through Safari:

1. Open Longfu88 in Safari.
2. Tap Share and choose Add to Home Screen.
3. Confirm Add.
