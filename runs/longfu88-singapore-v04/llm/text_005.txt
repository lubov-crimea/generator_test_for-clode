Players must be at least 18 years old and gamble only where it is lawful, which is the starting point of the responsible-gambling policy. Beyond the age limit, the main control is self-exclusion, which is requested by e-mail to customer support at support@longfu88.com. The processing time is within 24 hours after the self-exclusion request is processed, and account access and gambling may be restricted for the agreed period. Account closure is a separate route that also runs through customer support.

The procedure has four short steps: contact support and request self-exclusion or account closure, confirm the account details and the preferred exclusion terms, allow the casino to restrict or close access, and seek independent support where appropriate. Self-assessment questions help players identify possible gambling-related harm, so answering them honestly is a sensible first check. This page cannot replace independent help, and players can turn to these organisations:

- GamCare
- Gamblers Anonymous
- Gambling Therapy
