VIP points come from eligible real-money gameplay, and in the Longfu88 VIP Program those points unlock numbered reward levels. There are 25 levels, grouped into five tiers: Bronze, Silver, Gold, Platinum and Diamond. Higher VIP levels raise withdrawal limits and cashback, and from level 4 a personal VIP manager joins the personalised offers that come with the climb. Six steps of the ladder show how the figures develop:

| Level | Points | Reward | Cashback | Withdrawal limit |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 1 | €9.8 | 1% | €9,800 |
| 5 | 70 | €24.5 | 2% | €34,300 |
| 10 | 700 | €147 | 4% | €63,700 |
| 15 | 2,100 | €392 | 6% | €88,200 |
| 20 | 3,600 | €637 | 8% | €117,600 |
| 25 | 5,500 | €980 | 10% | €147,000 |

Reward and withdrawal limits may differ by VIP level and currency, and level-based benefits and promotion eligibility may change under programme rules. A player who plans to rely on a higher limit should therefore confirm the current terms for the level reached.
