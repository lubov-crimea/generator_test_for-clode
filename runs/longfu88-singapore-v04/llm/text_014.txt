Mobile play is a responsive website on smartphone and tablet browsers, and the platforms Android, iOS and mobile web are all covered. The Longfu88 app is the home-screen version of that site, a shortcut that gives quick access to the casino and sportsbook. Mobile access covers the casino, the sportsbook, account access, deposits, withdrawals and bonuses, so routine account tasks can be done from a phone.

On Android, the shortcut is added from the Chrome browser menu:

1. Open Longfu88 in Chrome.
2. Open browser menu and choose Add to Home screen.
3. Confirm the shortcut.

On iOS, the same shortcut is added through Safari:

1. Open Longfu88 in Safari.
2. Tap Share and choose Add to Home Screen.
3. Confirm Add.
