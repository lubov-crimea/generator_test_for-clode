=== b04 ===
Registration is done by e-mail and takes five steps, which are completed in this order:

1. Open Longfu88 and select Register.
2. Enter email and create a password.
3. Confirm age and acceptance of Terms and Conditions and Privacy Policy.
4. Complete the additional personal details and residential address.
5. Select Create Account and complete any requested email confirmation.

Registration requires an email, a password, a legal name, a date of birth and a residential address, and every player may hold only one personal account. A lost password is therefore no reason to open a second one.

Returning players select Login on the website, enter the registered email and password and complete any security check. The Longfu88 login uses exactly the details given at registration, and a player who searches for the Longfu caino login page is looking for this same option. After a forgotten password, Forgot Password on the login screen sends a reset link to the registered email, and the emailed instructions set a new one.

=== b05 ===
KYC verification is the identity check that links an account to a real person. Longfu88 may request it when an account is under review, before a withdrawal, on unusual or high-value transactions and after changes to account or payment details. Withdrawals can be held until the checks are done, so a player who expects to cash out soon gains by sending documents early.

The accepted documents fall into four groups:

- Identity: passport, national identity card or driving licence.
- Address: utility bill or bank statement.
- Payment method: bank or card ownership evidence, or digital wallet ownership evidence.
- Source of funds, when requested: proof of income, bank statements or other supporting financial documents.

Documents may be sent within up to 30 days of the request. A complete document set is reviewed within up to 10 days of receipt, and a telephone or face check may be added. Failed checks can lead to a refused transaction, restrictions on the account or withdrawals withheld until compliance is settled.
