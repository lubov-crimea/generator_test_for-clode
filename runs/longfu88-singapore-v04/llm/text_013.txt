Customer support runs through two channels: live chat, which is available 24/7, and e-mail at support@longfu88.com. Live chat suits quick questions about a deposit, a bonus activation or a pending withdrawal, because the conversation happens during the session itself. E-mail fits matters that need a written record, such as a self-exclusion request or a document query. Support is offered in six languages: English, Malay, Simplified Chinese, Thai, Indonesian and Vietnamese, so players from Singapore can write in English or in Simplified Chinese.
