The Longfu88 register process accepts e-mail alone as the registration method, and it consists of five steps:

1. Open the site and pick Register.
2. Supply an e-mail address along with a freshly created password.
3. State that the player is of legal age, and take the Terms and Conditions and the Privacy Policy on board.
4. Other personal data and the residential address come next.
5. Hit Create Account and finish whichever e-mail confirmation is requested.

Before starting, it helps to keep the birth date, legal name and residential address within reach, since the form also needs an e-mail and a password. Eligibility is tied to age: the minimum for registering is 18, and whatever gambling age the relevant jurisdiction sets must be met too. Per player, one personal account is permitted at Longfu88, which rules out a second one.
