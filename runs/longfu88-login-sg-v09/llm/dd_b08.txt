An account may be denominated in USDT, EUR, SGD, USD, THB or MYR, six currencies in all. Longfu88 allows each player one personal account, in one of those currencies, and the terms tie three conditions to it: registration details have to be accurate, only the one personal account may be used, and third-party payment methods stay out. Currency makes no difference to these conditions, which cover the account as a whole. Separately, a resident must be allowed by law to gamble in the place where they live, which is the player's own matter and not something the account settles.
