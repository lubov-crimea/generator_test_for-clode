=== b03 ===
A Longfu login works with the registered e-mail address and password, which is the method for every player who holds an account at Longfu88. The sign-in runs in three steps:

1. Select Login on the website.
2. Enter the registered e-mail and password on the login screen.
3. Complete any security check and access the account.

A security check, when one is requested, comes before access, so the account opens only once that step is cleared. Beyond the sign-in itself, the registered e-mail matters for another reason: the same address receives the reset link described next.

=== b04 ===
A forgotten password can be replaced with a new one in three steps:

1. Choose Forgot Password on the login screen.
2. Request a reset link using the registered e-mail.
3. Follow the e-mailed instructions to set a new password.

A player who no longer has access to the registered mailbox should contact support. The contact details are in the support section further down the page.
