For account problems, customer support has live chat open every hour of the day, 24/7, and the e-mail address is support@longfu88.com. Questions that come up most often concern a failed login, verification documents to upload and a reset e-mail that has not arrived. Six languages are covered by support: Indonesian, Vietnamese, English, Simplified Chinese, Thai and Malay, so a description of the problem can be given in whichever is most familiar.
