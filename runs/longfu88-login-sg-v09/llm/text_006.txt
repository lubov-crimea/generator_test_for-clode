Withdrawals are the stage where identity and payment checks may be required, and payments may be held until identification, payment ownership and any required source-of-funds checks are complete. For a player at Longfu88 this means a withdrawal request can wait on those checks before it moves on. Withdrawal requests are generally processed within three working days, which describes ordinary handling and not a promised speed.

One rule applies to every request: the payment method must belong to the player, and third-party payment methods are not to be used. A player who plans to cash out can therefore keep the identity documents ready and use a payment method held in their own name, which covers the two checks that matter most before the payment is released.
