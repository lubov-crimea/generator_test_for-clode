Account holders at Longfu88 who need distance from gambling can choose self-exclusion or closure, and customer support receives the request in both cases. Self-exclusion can limit account access and gambling during an agreed period, so the break is bounded in time and not left open. Closure ends access, yet it is requested through support in the same manner. Both options sit within the rules on age and lawfulness: no one under 18 plays, and gambling is acceptable solely within what local law permits.

The request moves through four stages:

1. Support is contacted with the wish for self-exclusion or closure.
2. Exclusion terms are agreed and the account details confirmed.
3. Restriction or closure of access follows from the casino's side.
4. Outside help is considered where it fits.

Independent sources of help include Gambling Therapy and, alongside it, GamCare and Gamblers Anonymous, all suited to anyone preferring a conversation with people unconnected to the operator. Self-assessment questions are another tool, useful for noticing possible harm from gambling, and working through them is a sensible opening check before choosing between a break and closure.
