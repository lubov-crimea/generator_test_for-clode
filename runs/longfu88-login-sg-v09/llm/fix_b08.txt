An account at Longfu88 can use any of six currencies: EUR, USD, MYR, THB, SGD and USDT. A player holds one personal account in one of these, and the terms attach three requirements to it: register with accurate information, use only one personal account, and keep third-party payment methods out of it. These rules apply to the account as a whole, whichever of the currencies is used. Residents also need to be legally permitted to gamble in their place of residence, a point that stays with the player and is not settled by the account itself.
