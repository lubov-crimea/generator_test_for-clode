Contact and tracking each come with a choice that the player makes at Longfu88. Where promotions are concerned, unsubscribing from promotional communications is possible, as is adjusting direct-marketing preferences, and that is how news of bonus offers is stopped while the account remains open. Cookies are the other matter. The website uses four kinds, namely necessary, preference, analytics and marketing, and they support its functions and its measurement. Taken together, the two settings decide what messages reach the player and what the site records during a visit.
