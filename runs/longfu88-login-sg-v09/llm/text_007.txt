An account at Longfu88 can use any of six currencies: EUR, USD, MYR, THB, SGD and USDT. A player holds one personal account in one of these, and the terms attach three requirements to it: register with accurate information, use only one personal account, and keep third-party payment methods out of it. Accurate details matter because they are what the verification step later compares against the documents. Residents also need to be legally permitted to gamble in their place of residence, a point that stays with the player and is not settled by the account itself.
