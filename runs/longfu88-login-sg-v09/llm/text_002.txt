A player who wants a break from gambling, or wants to stop altogether, has two routes at Longfu88: self-exclusion and account closure. Both are requested through customer support. With self-exclusion, account access and gambling may be restricted for the agreed period, so the break has a defined length instead of staying open-ended. Account closure goes further and ends access, and it is requested in the same way. Players must be at least 18 years old and gamble only where lawful, which applies to anyone who keeps an account open as well as to those who step away.

In practice, the request follows four steps:

1. Contact support and request self-exclusion or account closure.
2. Confirm the account details and the preferred exclusion terms.
3. Allow the casino to restrict or close access.
4. Seek independent support where appropriate.

Independent organisations such as GamCare, Gamblers Anonymous and Gambling Therapy offer help outside the casino, which is useful when a player would rather talk to someone who is not connected to the operator. Self-assessment questions help players identify possible gambling-related harm, and working through them is a practical first check before deciding between a break and closure.
