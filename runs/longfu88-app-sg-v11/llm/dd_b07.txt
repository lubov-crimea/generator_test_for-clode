The lobby of the mobile site groups the games into nine categories, ready for a phone or tablet player to choose from:

- Crash and instant games
- Lottery
- Jackpots
- Slots
- Live dealer games
- Fishing
- Fast games
- Table games
- P2P games

Providers whose games appear include Evolution, Pragmatic Play, NetEnt and Hacksaw Gaming, and six further studios supply titles as well.
