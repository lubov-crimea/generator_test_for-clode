Account verification is a routine identity check. It may be requested during a review of the account, when a withdrawal is pending, after transactions that look unusual or large, and whenever the account details or the payment details are changed. Documents that can be requested:

- A government-issued photo ID or a passport
- A document linking the payment method to the player
- Proof of residence for the registered address
- Source-of-funds or source-of-wealth paperwork, when asked

Anything requested is to be submitted within up to 30 days.
