Responsible gaming on the mobile website rests on one rule first: players must be at least 18 years old and gamble only where lawful. Self-assessment questions help players identify possible gambling-related harm, and an account can be closed by requesting account closure through customer support. Where self-exclusion applies, account access and gambling may be restricted for the agreed period. Independent organisations such as GamCare, Gamblers Anonymous and Gambling Therapy offer further support.
