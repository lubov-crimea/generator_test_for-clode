=== b03 ===
The Longfu88 website works in the browser of a smartphone or tablet. A home-screen shortcut is added once and then opens the casino with a single tap. Nobody needs a Longfu88 apk or any other installation file: the casino opens in the mobile browser and sits on the home screen as a shortcut, with no store listing. The next two sub-sections give the steps for Android in Chrome and for iOS in Safari.

=== b04 ===
Android phones use Chrome to put a shortcut of the casino website on the home screen, and it takes three steps:

1. Open the casino website in Chrome.
2. Open the browser menu and choose Add to Home screen.
3. Confirm the shortcut.

The shortcut then appears among the apps of the phone.

=== b05 ===
On an iPhone or iPad, Safari creates the shortcut of the casino website, and the Share button is the starting point:

1. Open the casino website in Safari.
2. Tap Share and choose Add to Home Screen.
3. Confirm Add.

The new icon then sits on the home screen of the iPhone or iPad.
