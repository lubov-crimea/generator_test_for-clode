Before anything else, the mobile website asks for two things: that the player has turned 18, and that gambling happens only in places where it is lawful. Self-assessment questionnaires help spot signs of possible harm from gambling. A player who wants to stop can ask customer support to close the account. If self-exclusion is in force, the account and gambling can be restricted throughout the agreed period. Further help comes from outside bodies including GamCare, and also Gambling Therapy and Gamblers Anonymous.
