At Longfu88 the only way to register is by e-mail, and five details go into the form:

- Date of birth
- Password
- Legal name
- E-mail
- Residential address

Signing in from a phone matches signing in on a desktop: after Login is selected, the password is entered alongside the e-mail that the account was opened with, and any security check is completed. A forgotten password is dealt with through Forgot Password on that screen, and a separate login page describes recovery in more detail.
