- **Can a player log in to Longfu88 from a phone?** Yes, in the browser of a smartphone or tablet, with a home-screen shortcut as an option.
- **How does the login work?** It uses the registered e-mail and password.
- **What follows a forgotten password?** A reset link is requested with the registered e-mail.
- **Where can help be found if the login still fails?** Customer support answers through live chat 24/7 or by e-mail.
- **Which languages does support speak?** English, Malay, Simplified Chinese, Thai, Indonesian and Vietnamese.
