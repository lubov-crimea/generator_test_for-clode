The account holder is responsible for safeguarding the login credentials, that is the e-mail address and the password that open the account. Since the login relies on these two details alone, a password that has been shared or lost should be replaced through the reset described above.

The operator has its own part. Longfu88 applies technical and organisational safeguards to protect accounts, payments and personal information, and it may suspend activity for verification or suspected abuse. For the player, a suspension is therefore a matter for support and not something a new password can solve.
