The first withdrawal is where an account meets its checks. Identity and payment checks may be required for access to withdrawals, and Longfu88 may hold payments until identification, payment ownership and any required source-of-funds checks are complete. A player who has not yet sent the requested documents may therefore notice the hold at the cashier rather than at the login screen.

Withdrawal requests are generally processed within three working days, a general timeframe and not a promise for any single payment. The payment method must also belong to the player, because third-party payment methods are not allowed. If the checks are failed, the outcome can be a refused transaction, account restrictions or withheld withdrawals until the player complies, so it pays to send complete and readable documents the first time.
