The account holder answers for the safety of the login credentials, which means the e-mail on file and the password that goes with it. With only these two details guarding the account, a password that was shared or lost ought to be replaced through the reset covered earlier.

The operator carries a share too. Longfu88 secures personal data, payments and accounts through technical and organisational safeguards, and it may halt activity while a verification takes place or when abuse is suspected. A suspension is therefore a job for support, since a new password does not lift it.
