A first withdrawal is when an account runs into its checks. Before money can leave the account, identity checks and payment checks may be needed, and Longfu88 is entitled to hold a payout while it confirms who the player is, whom the payment method belongs to and, if needed, where the money comes from. A player who has not yet supplied the requested documents may notice the hold at the cashier instead of at the login screen.

Processing of withdrawal requests usually completes within three working days. That period is a general guide rather than a guarantee for each individual payment. Payment methods that belong to third parties are not accepted, so the one used must be owned by the player. Failed checks can end in a declined transaction, restrictions on the account or withdrawals that stay withheld until the player complies.
