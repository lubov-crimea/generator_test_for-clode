A player without an account registers at Longfu88 by e-mail, which is the only registration method given, and the process takes five steps:

1. Open the site and select Register.
2. Enter an e-mail address and create a password.
3. Confirm age and acceptance of the Terms and Conditions and the Privacy Policy.
4. Complete the additional personal details and the residential address.
5. Select Create Account and complete any requested e-mail confirmation.

Registration requires an e-mail address, a password, a legal name, a date of birth and a residential address. The player must be 18 or over and of legal gambling age in the relevant jurisdiction, and only one personal account is allowed per player, so someone who already has an account and has lost the password should use the reset instead of registering again.
