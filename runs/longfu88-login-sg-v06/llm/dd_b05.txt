Longfu88 does not ask for documents at every login, only on specific occasions: during an account review, ahead of a withdrawal, for transactions seen as unusual or high-value, and after an alteration of the account data or the payment data. The evidence that counts varies with the check:

- A national ID card, a passport or a driving licence proves identity.
- Where the player lives is evidenced by a bank statement, or by a utility bill if that suits better.
- Ownership of the payment method is shown by bank or card evidence, or by wallet evidence in the case of digital wallets.
- Source-of-funds checks accept income proof, bank statements or similar supporting financial documents, though only when requested.

Documents must arrive within 30 days of being requested, and holding them back until the final day brings no advantage. Going through a full set of papers takes up to 10 days as a rule. Until that ends, an account being checked may remain on hold, which can delay access.
