Three rules in the account terms can restrict access even when nothing goes wrong at the login screen. First, long inactivity may lead to a review or closure of the account under the applicable terms, so a rarely used account benefits from an occasional visit. Second, disagreement with a dispute or account decision is taken to customer support with a request for a review. Third, promotional and contractual terms can be changed by Longfu88, with the updates published on the site, which makes checking the terms now and then a sensible habit.
