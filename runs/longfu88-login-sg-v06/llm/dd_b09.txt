Longfu88 offers e-mail as its only registration method, and five stages complete it:

1. Head to the site and press Register.
2. Provide the player's e-mail together with a chosen password.
3. The player first confirms age and then gives consent to the Privacy Policy plus the Terms and Conditions.
4. Residential address and further personal details are entered.
5. The final move is Create Account, followed by whatever e-mail confirmation the site asks for.

Registration collects five items. Among them are the date of birth, residential address and legal name, besides e-mail and password. Eighteen is the lowest age accepted, and registrants must also have reached the gambling age legal in their own jurisdiction. Each player gets one personal account only. For that reason, a returning player who forgot the password should go through the reset route rather than register a second time.
