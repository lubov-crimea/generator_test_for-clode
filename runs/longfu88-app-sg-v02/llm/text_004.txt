On a phone or tablet, the first line of protection is the player. The account holder is responsible for safeguarding the login credentials, so they stay private on every device, and a phone should not be passed to others while it is unlocked. On the operator side, Longfu88 applies technical and organisational safeguards to protect accounts, payments and personal information. Activity on an account may also be suspended while verification is carried out or abuse is suspected. Everyday access runs through the login page of the site, from the shortcut as well as from a browser tab.
