At the first layer stands the account holder, whose duty is to keep the login credentials safe and private on every device, a phone or tablet included. One general habit helps: an unlocked phone should not be handed to other people. The operator covers its side too, since Longfu88 applies safeguards that are both technical and organisational to personal information, payments and accounts. Verification checks and suspected abuse are both reasons for which account activity may be suspended. Everyday entry goes through the login page, which behaves the same from a shortcut as from a browser tab.
