Opening an account from a phone takes five steps, and the credentials are an e-mail address plus a password.

1. Tap Register on the website.
2. Give an email and choose a password.
3. Accept the Terms and Conditions, accept the Privacy Policy and confirm age.
4. Add the other personal details, including the residential address.
5. Finally, Create Account is chosen, and any email confirmation request gets an answer.

The residential address, date of birth and legal name are requested too, and the same e-mail plus password serve for later logins.
