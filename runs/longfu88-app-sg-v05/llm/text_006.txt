A player registers with an e-mail address and a password in five steps.

1. Open the website and select Register.
2. Enter an email and create a password.
3. Confirm age and acceptance of Terms and Conditions and Privacy Policy.
4. Complete the additional personal details and residential address.
5. Select Create Account and complete any requested email confirmation.

Required details are an e-mail, a password, a legal name, a date of birth and a residential address, and the same e-mail and password are used for login afterwards.
