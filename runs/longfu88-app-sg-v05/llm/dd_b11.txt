Longfu88 provides customer support, yet many phone issues can be solved at once. A missing shortcut is fixed by doing the setup again, with Chrome on Android or Safari on iOS. When a page fails to open, a refresh comes first, along with confirming that the internet connection works. For failed logins, the e-mail address should be verified and Forgot Password tried on the sign-in screen. Support is the last stop: e-mail is one route, and live chat, open around the clock (24/7), is the other.
