Longfu88 needs a browser on a phone or tablet, nothing more, and the shortcut that lands on the home screen is made once, not at each visit. There is no installation file to fetch and no Longfu88 apk, as the website runs in the browser of the mobile device and may stay on the home screen as an icon. Setup is brief: three steps for each system, done once per device. Two sub-sections follow, one for Android (Chrome) and one for iOS (Safari).
