The cashier is built into the mobile site, so a phone can send a withdrawal request in six steps.

1. Open the cashier after logging in.
2. Choose Withdrawal, then a supported payment method or destination.
3. State the sum and where it goes: a blockchain address, a wallet or the receiving bank.
4. Verify identity and payment method if the casino asks.
5. Check the request, then confirm it.
6. The funds arrive after the casino has processed the request, and after the settlement of a bank or network, where there is one.

Casino processing generally takes up to three working days.
