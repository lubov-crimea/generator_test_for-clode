Looking after the login credentials, whatever the device, is the responsibility of the account holder. A general tip is not to leave the device unlocked in other hands. Longfu88 for its part runs technical and organisational protections for personal information, payments and accounts, while suspension of activity by the operator is possible for verification or suspected abuse. The two duties are separate, so the safeguards of the operator do not remove the need for care with credentials on the holder's side.
