Registration is by e-mail only, and the account created that way is the one a player opens from a phone. Longfu88 asks for the same login on a phone as on a computer: the player selects Login on the website, enters the registered e-mail and password and completes any security check to reach the account. A player who forgot the password selects Forgot Password on the login screen. The method is e-mail and password, so a phone needs no separate account.
