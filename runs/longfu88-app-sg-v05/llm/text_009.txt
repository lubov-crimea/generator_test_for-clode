Most phone problems have a plain fix, and Longfu88 offers customer support for the rest. A missing shortcut is fixed by repeating the steps in Chrome on Android or in Safari on iOS. When a page does not load, a reload and a check of the internet connection come first. Login failures call for a check of the e-mail address and Forgot Password on the login screen. If a problem remains after these checks, support is reachable by 24/7 live chat or by e-mail.
