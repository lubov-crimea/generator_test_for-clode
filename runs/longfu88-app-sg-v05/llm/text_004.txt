=== b04 ===
Longfu88 works in the browser of a smartphone or tablet, and a home-screen shortcut is added once, not on every visit. Nobody needs an installation file or a Longfu88 apk, because the website opens in the mobile browser and sits on the home screen as a shortcut. The setup is short, three steps per system, and it is done once per device. Two sub-sections follow, with the steps for Android (Chrome) and for iOS (Safari).

=== b05 ===
On an Android phone, Chrome adds a shortcut of the casino website to the home screen in three short steps. Once confirmed, the shortcut appears among the apps of the phone and opens the same website.

1. Open the casino website in Chrome.
2. Choose Add to Home screen in the browser menu.
3. Confirm the shortcut.

=== b06 ===
On an iPhone or iPad, Safari does the same job through the Share menu: it places a shortcut of the casino website on the home screen. The icon then opens the same site as the browser tab.

1. Open the casino website in Safari.
2. Tap Share and choose Add to Home Screen.
3. Confirm Add.
