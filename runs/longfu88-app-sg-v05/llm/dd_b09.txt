Registration works by e-mail alone, so a phone opens the very account that was created that way. Longfu88 keeps the sign-in routine identical to the computer one: the player goes to the website, picks Login and types the registered e-mail along with the password, then clears any security check to get into the account. If the password slips the mind, the login screen carries a Forgot Password option. No separate phone account exists because e-mail and password are the whole method.
