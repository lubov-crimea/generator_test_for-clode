Two settings stay in the player's hands. Direct-marketing preferences can be managed, and promotional communications from Longfu88 can be unsubscribed from, so a player decides how much marketing material reaches the registered e-mail.

The site also sets cookies of four kinds: necessary, preference, analytics and marketing cookies. Together they support website functions and measurement, and the names of the four kinds show which cookies belong to the running of the site and which to its measurement and marketing.
