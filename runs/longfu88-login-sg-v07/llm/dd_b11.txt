Customer support covers questions about the account, login and verification. Reaching support is possible by e-mail, at support@longfu88.com, and by live chat, which stays available 24/7, day and night. Typical reasons to write are a failed login, a missing reset e-mail and the sending of verification documents. Messages can be written in Malay, English, Simplified Chinese, Thai, Vietnamese or Indonesian, so everyone can use a language that is familiar.
