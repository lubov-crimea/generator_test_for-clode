Responsible gambling starts with the age limit: players must be at least 18 years old and gamble only where it is lawful. Self-exclusion is requested by e-mail to customer support at support@longfu88.com, and account access and gambling may then be restricted for the agreed period. The self-exclusion is handled within 24 hours after the request is processed.

Account closure also goes through customer support. The procedure is short: contact support and ask for self-exclusion or closure, confirm the account details and the preferred exclusion terms, allow the casino to restrict or close access, and seek independent support where appropriate. Self-assessment questions help players identify possible gambling-related harm, and this page cannot replace independent help. Players can seek help from these organisations:

- GamCare
- Gamblers Anonymous
- Gambling Therapy
