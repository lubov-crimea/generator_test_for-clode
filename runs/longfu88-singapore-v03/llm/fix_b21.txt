Customer support has two channels: live chat, which is available 24/7, and email support at support@longfu88.com. Live chat gives an answer in the middle of a session, while email leaves a written record of a request. Support speaks English, Malay, Simplified Chinese, Thai, Indonesian and Vietnamese, so most players from Singapore can write in a language they are comfortable with. A player with an account problem can therefore start in chat at any hour and follow up by email if needed.
