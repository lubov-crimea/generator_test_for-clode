Live chat and e-mail, both available 24/7, handle questions about accounts, logins and verification. For a failed login, live chat is the suitable channel, while support@longfu88.com is the address for a reset e-mail that never shows up or for sending a document. Conversations are possible in six languages: Indonesian, Vietnamese, Malay, Thai, English and Simplified Chinese. The registered e-mail address is worth adding to any message.
