Longfu88 accepts new accounts through a single channel, e-mail, and the whole sign-up consists of five stages:

1. From the website, choose Register.
2. Provide an e-mail address together with a new password.
3. Agree to the Privacy Policy and the Terms and Conditions, and confirm the player's age.
4. Enter further personal data, with the home address among it.
5. Select Create Account, then complete the e-mail confirmation when one is requested.

The form collects five items in total: legal name, date of birth, home address, e-mail and password, so keeping them to hand before the first stage is practical. Registration is open to people aged 18 and above, provided the minimum age for gambling under the relevant jurisdiction is reached too. One personal account is permitted per player, which means a single set of details is linked to a single person.
