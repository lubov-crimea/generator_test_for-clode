Verification requests arise in four cases: unusual or high-value transactions, the review of an account, an impending withdrawal, and any update to the account or to payment details. These are the documents that can be asked for:

- Government photo identification, or alternatively a passport
- Confirmation of the residential address by a document
- Proof that the payment method belongs to the player
- On request, papers that explain the origin of the money and assets

Documents asked for by the casino must reach it within up to 30 days. A full set of documents is typically reviewed in up to 10 days.
