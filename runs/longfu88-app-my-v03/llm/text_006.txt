Four common phone problems with Longfu88 have a plain fix, and customer support covers whatever the checks leave open:

- Shortcut missing: repeat the steps in Chrome on Android or in Safari on iOS, then look for the icon on the home screen.
- Page not loading: reload it and check the internet connection.
- Login failing: check the e-mail address, then request a reset link with Forgot Password on the login screen.
- Problem remaining: contact support through live chat, available 24/7, or by e-mail.
