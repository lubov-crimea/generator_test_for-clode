Most trouble on a phone has a short cause and a short cure, so it helps to go through the same four checks before asking anyone. Longfu88 support steps in for whatever the table does not solve.

| Problem | Fix |
|---|---|
| The shortcut does not appear | Repeat the steps in Chrome on Android or in Safari on iOS |
| The page does not load | Reload it and check the internet connection |
| The login fails | Check the e-mail address and use Forgot Password on the login screen |
| The problem remains | Contact support through live chat, 24/7, or by e-mail |
